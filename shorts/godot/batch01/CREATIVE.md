# Five Godot music edits — batch 01 direction

The user approved the monochrome R3 proof on 2026-10-05 and asked for five more Shorts, more doodles/shading, famous music and a distinct idea for each. These are production boards, not a claim that the batch has been rendered or reviewed. Final timing must follow the downloaded music sections and measured/auditioned accents.

All five retain the approved thin contour / pale paper treatment, manually authored editable Godot artwork, one stable premise, legible phone-size faces, actual viewpoint drawings, held ink and brief music-led reframes. They are music edits without narration. Clothing and character identity remain recognizable. New art belongs under `shorts/`; existing storytime art, rigs, episodes and renders are preserved.

## Shared execution choices

- Aim for 12–16 seconds and 8–10 legible drawings/reframes. Let important poses hold for roughly a musical phrase instead of firing an effect at every kick.
- Cut the image and pose together on an audible accent; anticipation may lead by 2–4 frames, and a punch/whip settles in 5–8 frames. Save exact timing after selecting the music. The relative landmarks below are planning positions, not verified musical downbeats.
- Shade the underside of hair, sleeve folds, hoodie pocket, skirt folds and shirt collar with deliberate gray patches and short crosshatching. Draw shadows once; do not regenerate noise every frame. Keep facial whites clean.
- Each short has one accent family. Doodles occupy negative space and never obscure a face, hand or the single premise. Limit their appearance to authored events, then hold or disappear with the next drawing.
- A physical prop is attached to an authored hand/contact point in its held pose. Use cuts between separate held poses unless a continuous hand path has actually been authored. Do not slide a held phone or pencil through a hand to fake handling.
- Use one brief SFX only when an actual visual event needs it; source it from the existing original recorded MP3/category assets. Music remains the main rhythm. No synthesized effects or character dialogue.
