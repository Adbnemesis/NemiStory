# Character direction for ink Shorts

This is the music-edit application of ADB and Nemi's personalities. The approved ink drawings and current version-3 art are the visual authority for this pipeline. Older storytime documents describe different costumes, proportions and hold lengths; do not import those drawings or pacing rules into the approved Shorts style. Preserve all original episode and rig source.

| Direction | ADB | Nemi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting attitude | Relaxed confidence, economical gestures, dry wit | Observant curiosity, creative excitement, self-aware overthinking |
| Cute contrast | A compliment, discovery or mistake briefly breaks composure; he recovers with a small deliberate gesture | A little idea becomes an enthusiastic project; realization brings a shy or deadpan reaction |
| Eyes and head | A precise side glance leads a restrained head turn; chin and shoulder settle clearly | Eyes notice the object first; head tilt and tucked/open hands make the thought readable |
| Hand vocabulary | Point, glasses adjustment, small wave, phone check, composed open hand | Pencil/sketchbook grip, headphone touch, peace, wave, shy cover, proud presentation |
| Doodle character | Cleaner angular brackets, rays, restrained zigzags, graphic ink accents | Softer stars, flowers, hearts, clouds, leaves and playful page marks |
| Failure to avoid | Turning every beat into manic panic or copying Nemi's exact performance | Treating Nemi as a generic sweet mascot or spamming unrelated cute icons |

These are editorial tendencies, not exclusive action or color lists. Read [V3_SCHEMA.md](V3_SCHEMA.md) for the actual supported visible actions, motion fields and event kinds. A different palette or doodle is welcome when the premise warrants it. Canonical details such as ADB's engineering, anime, gaming and table-tennis interests, or Nemi's singing, art, anime and awkward observations can provide a premise without inventing new biography.

For a solo short, make the visible choice and consequence understandable without narration: noticing a favorite beat → committing to the gesture → being caught, or a confident plan → small mistake → dignified recovery. A reaction montage still needs a cause. Use one stable premise line when text helps; do not replace the visual sequence with moving story captions. Nemi speaks and writes in first person rather than gendered self-descriptions.

For a duo short, ADB and Nemi are visible creative peers. Give each a distinct response to the same object, song or challenge, then a shared resolution. Let one notice first and the other answer, or alternate the lead before they match. Their mere presence together does not establish relationship canon. Keep screen direction, relative scale, eyelines and any shared prop consistent. A mirrored solo drawing is not a newly authored profile or physical exchange.

Motion follows the thought: eye lead, finite head/torso response, authored gesture, brief readable settle. On the current Shorts route, no fully static image interval may exceed 1.5 seconds, and total duration is 12–25 seconds. Meet this with new poses, expression changes, attached hand/prop articulation, camera travel or meaningful finite events. Tiny jitter, permanent bobbing and blinking alone do not supply the requested dynamism. Completed ink does not crawl or randomly redraw.

Contact drawings have limits. Keep held phone/pencil/sketchbook geometry locked to the fingers throughout supported gesture motion. Cut between independently authored contact poses when no continuous exchange exists. Do not fake pickup, walking or handoffs with floating root movement. Add a missing action in the separate Shorts art/director only, update schema and a runnable example in the same change, then inspect its contact boundaries in the actual exported frames.
