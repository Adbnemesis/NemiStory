## Pose microaction and camera grammar

Use a continuous thought path through each pose, not a static card plus a generic jitter filter. Eyes notice first; head follows in3–6frames; chest/shoulder/support responds next; the supported hand/prop finishes and the thought settles. One concentration breath, one foot-support compression, one chin decision or one eyeline answer is enough when it actually changes the feeling. Held ink geometry stays coherent. Feet do not slide; book/phone/pencil and their grips share exactly the same sampled movement.

Camera has two separate jobs. A **finite base move** quietly follows attention through the current pose: approximately1–2% push/pull for frame-safe whole-body images,3–4% for an intentional close/detail crop, or a10–24pixel screen pan toward the thought’s object. A **selected attack impulse** is small (initial candidate0.6–1% scale,2–4frame entry and6–10frame release) and belongs to a real important source attack. Never apply it to all flux peaks. Recompute hand/foot/premise margins before using these amplitudes; root’s inspected palm-safe art is the constraint. Match or wipe transitions should continue camera momentum rather than finishing and leaving a perceptually dead slab before the next card.

ADB and Nemi keep different amplitudes and timing. In shared shots a relevant Nemi response can be warmer, while ADB answers with about half the internal amplitude2–3frames later. A choice made by one character must cause the other’s next action. Difference in amplitude is a direction starting point, not a universal automated bounce.

All recipes below are conceptual director instructions. They are not unvalidated private fields added to the production schema. Translate only into the maintained new supported engine API after its implementation is established.
