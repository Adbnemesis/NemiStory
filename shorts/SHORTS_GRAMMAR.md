# Ink Shorts grammar

Follow [the pose-first workflow](godot/V3_WORKFLOW.md), [strict schema](godot/V3_SCHEMA.md) and cast skill direction. [Motion04](review/motion04/index.html) is the current review target. Read the relevant actual-reference motion notes for [ref01](review/motion04/ref01/MOTION.md) or [ref03](review/motion04/ref03/MOTION.md) before directing a new edit. This grammar is artistic context, not a second renderer.

- Show a recognisable character/posture/situation in the first second; no intro logo or title card.
- Use12–25s chosen for the music phrase and payoff. No fully static image exceeds1.5s. A fast change earns attention by revealing a meaningful new thought, silhouette, reaction or object detail.
- Plan thought and whole-body pose cards, then give each a small coherent internal-action path and camera purpose. Want → visible choice → consequence/reversal → payoff supplies a causal sequence. More poses alone are not a story.
- ADB’s composed baseline meets a deliberate dry reaction; Nemi’s curious creative choice meets a warm or self-aware response. Duo singles must affect the partner’s next shared beat.
- Show major silhouette changes with grounded feet. Contrast full-body images with purposeful face, prop or single-character frames, then return wider. A crop is not another pose; a rotated front drawing is not a profile.
- Use source accents for arrivals and record anticipation separately. An early transition start is not the same as the target pose landing. Quantize once to30fps, preserving sourceStart/hash and candidate uncertainty.
- Keep most cuts clean. Choose a finite smear/whip/match/focus wipe where attention changes; a match needs real framing/scale/focus contrast. Let the target drawing read after the transition.
- Eyes lead a subtle head/grounded shoulder response through the held thought. A directed camera push/pull/pan can keep that stable art developing between cuts; selected real music attacks can add small finite impulses. Avoid generic periodic hand/sleeve loops, body bob, random line redraws or empty drift. Keep supported grips intact; do not fake walking, pickups or exchanges.
- Trace chosen doodles once, then hold their finished geometry in world space; a camera move need not freeze their screen pixels. Sparse finite VFX surround the audience focus without obscuring the face/prop. No flash/spin/shake stack or effects quota.
- One small stable premise can clarify the situation. The actual pose/prop/expression carries the edit; no narration captions or dialogue by default.
- Choose music for the exact topic/energy/phrase. Record artist/version/source/range/hash/gain. Recognisable music is not proof of a current trend rank.
- Link exact recorded root/category SFX to named causal events. Verify the exported attacks and balance; do not synthesize replacements or make meme vocals character dialogue.
- A meaningful ending may restart semantically. A visual reset does not prove a seamless audio loop. Save first/final observations honestly.
- Inspect native settled poses, internal actions, camera extrema/impulses and transition start/mid/end, then decoded picture/audio/pacing and the complete export at real speed with sound. Verify that the duration ends with the useful thought rather than repeated solved poses. Source intent, proof inspection and whole-clip playback are distinct evidence.
- Preserve r1 source/review/movie during recuts and select a replacement only after QA. Audience retention/CTR requires release data; no validator or movement quota guarantees it.
