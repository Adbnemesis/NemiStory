# Ink Shorts grammar

The executable authority is [V3_SCHEMA.md](godot/V3_SCHEMA.md) and [V3_WORKFLOW.md](godot/V3_WORKFLOW.md), with author-specific skill direction. This grammar is reference-derived art context, not a substitute renderer.

- Start with a readable character/action/situation. No intro logo or title card.
- Use 12–25 seconds chosen around the musical phrase and payoff. No static image over 1.5 seconds. A quick cut must reveal a meaningful new pose, thought, reaction or object detail.
- One audience focus per thought. A cute causal sequence has a want, choice, consequence and payoff. Pose quantity alone is not a story.
- ADB stays composed, shows a precise gesture/detail, then gives a small dry response. Nemi looks first, actively explores, reacts warmly and recovers. Duo leads alternate and affect the partner's next thought.
- Actual source accents govern landings. Quantize scene times once to30fps; retain sourceStart and hashes. Measured onsets are candidates, not automatically downbeats.
- Let eyes lead, head/hand follow, gesture settle. Author finite paths with matching hold keys; no endlessly bobbing character or jittering camera.
- Use medium/full framing alongside face/hand details. Keep feet visible when grounding matters and prop grips readable. Real profile/back drawings, never rotated front art labeled profile.
- Draw selected doodles once, then hold their completed ink. Moving particles/wipes finish within their named event. Use a meaningful transition device, not a stack of spin/flash/shake effects.
- One small stable premise. Actual pose/prop/expression carries the edit; no timed narration captions or dialogue by default.
- Choose music for topic/energy and musical phrase. Record artist/version/source/range/hash/gain. Keep current-trend claims separate from topic-fit judgments.
- SFX comes from exact root/category recordings, linked to named events and checked in exported sound. Do not synthesize substitutes or turn meme vocals into character dialogue.
- A resolved ending may restart semantically. A visual reset is not proof of a seamless audio loop. Record ending/first-frame observations honestly.
- Inspect first/final frames, full-body and contact shots, event/gesture boundaries and actual native playback. Decode final picture/audio/pacing independently. Audience retention/CTR is measured only after real posting.
