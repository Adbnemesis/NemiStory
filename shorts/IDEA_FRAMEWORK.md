# Ink Shorts idea direction

Start from one recognizable want or friction. Give it one visible choice and consequence, then a cute payoff that the music helps land. ADB's composure can meet an awkward detail; Nemi's creative enthusiasm can outrun her small intention; the duo can reach a shared moment through different responses. Creative peers do not automatically establish relationship canon.

Save promise/want/choice/consequence/payoff and one focus per thought in DIRECTION.md. Choose 12–25 seconds around a source musical phrase. Reject a premise that needs unexplained lore, text over idle art, identical reactions all the way through, meaningless camera motion or an ending added solely to hit15s.

The current motion/music direction ledger is [motion04/BEAT_DIRECTION.md](review/motion04/BEAT_DIRECTION.md); its final manifest is written after export/playback review. [Revision03 direction](review/revision03/DIRECTION.md) remains interim pose work. The previous [upgrade02 manifest](review/upgrade02/MANIFEST.json) remains history. Compare the last five premises, first images, props/stages, music versions/source sections, whole-body silhouettes, internal thought motion, framing/camera development, payoff types and transition devices. Change the thought and at least two visual dimensions. Palette and effects changes alone cannot make a repeated story unique.

The eight current ideas range from headphone confidence and a wrong classroom to a growing doodle, unequal musical energy, camera composure, brainfreeze/inspiration, a sleepy-cat daydream and a mutually chosen matching-pose photo. The three skills supply character-specific direction and an executable checked route; each new production must still earn its own story and rhythm.

Editorial questions: can a new viewer read the first second, see what the character wants, follow the choice/consequence and understand the final gesture? Does the music support the exact visual idea? Do whole-body, face and prop details earn their framing? Do a transition’s anticipation and target arrival support the thought rather than hide it? Does the camera continue attention through each held pose? Do selected tiny impacts correspond to the actual source music rather than arbitrary flux? Can the solved ending be trimmed without losing its readable payoff? Does the restart make sense? These judgments are review criteria, not predictions of views or shares.
