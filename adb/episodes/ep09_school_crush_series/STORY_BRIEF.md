# ADB EP09: School Crush Series (Complete 4-Part Compilation)

## 1. Overview & Direction Brief

* **Promise**: A complete 10-year adolescent romance saga tracing Aiden's journey with his school crush from primary school innocence to high school graduation closure.
* **Want**: Aiden navigates the awkward progression from childhood secret to adolescent ghosting, striving to understand his feelings and find closure without losing his dignity.
* **Choice**: He moves through the stages of trusting a friend with the secret, braving the middle-school double text, confessing after a high school math exam, and finally stepping off the pedestal when she dates someone else.
* **Consequence**: Each stage brings unexpected comedic turns—a broadcast betrayal, a mysterious year-long block, a two-word anticlimax ("I know"), and a shattered illusion.
* **Payoff**: Aiden graduates high school as genuine friends with his former crush, zero regrets, and steps into college ready for the real love of his life, Nemi.
