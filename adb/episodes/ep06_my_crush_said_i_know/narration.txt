A year or two after she blocked me, high school started. Sections got reshuffled again. But everyone taking Japanese as their second language got placed in the exact same class.

Which meant: me, my crush, and her boyfriend... all in one room. In middle school, the crush had faded. But seeing her every day? The feelings came right back.

At first, I was lonely. My middle school friends were in different sections, so I was just the quiet kid sitting alone in the back.

By next year, things turned around. I made great friends, got comfortable, and slowly started talking to my crush again, like normal classmates.

One day, after years of wondering, I finally asked the big question: 'Hey... why did you block me back then?'

She looked at me completely confused: 'Wait, I blocked you? I don't even remember that.' Bro. I spent years questioning my entire existence, and she didn't even remember.

After that, my brain thought there might still be a chance. So after our half-yearly math exam, I made my move: I was going to tell her I still liked her.

Exam over. Sweaty hands. I typed out the text, stared at the screen with my heart pounding, and hit send.

I waited, bracing for another block or a whole paragraph. My phone buzzed. Two words. She replied: 'I know.' ...That's it. 'I know.' High school was brutal, man.
