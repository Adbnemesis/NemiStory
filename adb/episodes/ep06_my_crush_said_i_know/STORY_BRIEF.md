# ADB Episode 06: "My Crush Said 'I Know'" — Story Brief & Direction

## 1. Core Direction Brief (Five Sentences)

* **Promise**: After enduring a humiliating block throughout middle school, Aiden enters high school only to find fate has locked him in the same classroom with his crush and her boyfriend.
* **Want**: Aiden wants to move past the awkwardness of the past, make friends, and eventually understand why she blocked him in the first place.
* **Choice**: After learning she doesn't even remember blocking him, Aiden seizes the post-math-exam emotional vulnerability to send a bold confession text letting her know he still likes her.
* **Consequence**: He waits in agonizing suspense, braced for an essay, a second block, or total disaster.
* **Payoff**: She casually hits back with a deadpan two-word reply—"I know"—delivering the ultimate anticlimactic high school mic drop.
