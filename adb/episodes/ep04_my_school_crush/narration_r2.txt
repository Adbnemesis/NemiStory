When I was eight years old, I transferred to a new primary school. Which is already pretty stressful when your entire worldview revolves around recess.

Back then, teachers had this policy to keep classes quiet: they forced opposite genders to sit together. Boy, girl, boy, girl, across every row. Their theory was that we'd be too awkward to talk. Flawless logic.

Except it did the exact opposite. Because I got seated with this girl. And no, before anyone types in the comments: she wasn't Nemi. I was eight years old, okay? Relax.

So there we were. Two eight-year-olds sitting at the same wooden double desk, side by side, for six hours a day, five days a week.

At first, it was the classic border dispute. You draw a pencil line down the center of the desk, and if someone's elbow crosses it, that's an international incident.

But when you spend every day right next to someone... sharing erasers, borrowing sharpeners, complaining about homework... borders start disappearing.

I don't know when or how it happened, but somewhere between third-grade math and lunch, I realized... I had a crush on my desk partner.

It was completely innocent. I didn't have a master plan. I was just eight years old, quietly happy that I got assigned to that desk.

Fast forward to the next year. We were nearing middle school, and everyone knew the classes were getting reshuffled. Different sections, different rooms, different seats.

One afternoon, a friend sat next to me and started drilling me: "Bro, who do you like? Tell me! I swear on my life I won't tell anyone, your secret is completely safe."

I was so stupid to believe him. I leaned in, whispered her name, and genuinely thought that secret was locked in a vault.

Within four minutes, this guy went and told every single friend in our group. And just to completely extinguish my soul, he walked right up to the girl and told her too.

I was standing in the hallway, completely paralyzed. The whole group was laughing, she knew, I knew she knew, and my soul basically left my body.

And then came the absolute cherry on top: I found out that another friend in our group ALSO liked the same girl. So now there were two of us, publicly exposed and mourning our childhood romance together.

So yeah. That was sad. But looking back on it now... at least I learned at age eight never to trust a friend with a secret.
