# ADB EP04 — Thumbnail Review & Mobile Audit

## Visual Legibility Audit

Both candidates were inspected across three standard YouTube viewport scales:
1. **Full Scale (1280×720)**: Desktop search results and watch page recommendations.
2. **Phone Scale (320×180)**: Mobile app feed cards and subscription views.
3. **Tiny Scale (160×90)**: Mobile sidebar recommendations and related video carousels.

---

### Concept A: "HE TOLD HER!"

- **Typography Legibility**:
  - Full (1280×720): Flawless contrast. The triple outline (black/red/black) ensures the white sans-serif lettering pops aggressively off the pale checkerboard background.
  - Phone (320×180): Instantly readable in under 0.2 seconds.
  - Tiny (160×90): Letters remain distinct with no outline bleeding or loss of counter space.
- **Character Recognizability**:
  - ADB's iconic middle-parted navy hair and facial contours are immediately identifiable.
  - The blushing cheeks and hands-to-face panic communicate the core emotion without needing to squint.
  - The whispering friend and the girl's braided ribbons establish the classroom triangle clearly.
- **Crop & Safe Zone Safety**:
  - The lower-right corner is clear of essential focal points, leaving space for YouTube's duration timestamp badge.

---

### Concept B: "YOU LIKE HER TOO?!"

- **Typography Legibility**:
  - Full (1280×720): High visual punch. Punctuation (`?!`) is crisp and dramatic.
  - Phone (320×180): High readability; the 4-word question fits naturally across the top third.
  - Tiny (160×90): Cleanly legible even at minimum thumbnail size.
- **Character Recognizability**:
  - ADB's deadpan horror on the left pairs hilariously with the crying comedic shock of his friend on the right.
  - The center notebook with the shared crush doodle ties directly to the third-act twist.
- **Crop & Safe Zone Safety**:
  - Lower-right corner has standard margin space for duration badge.
