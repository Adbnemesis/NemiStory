When I was eight years old, I transferred to a new primary school. Which is already pretty stressful when your whole world is recess.

Back then, teachers forced opposite genders to sit together to keep classes quiet. So I sat with a girl. And no, she wasn't Nemi. I was eight years old, okay? Relax.

We were two kids stuck at the same double desk all day. You draw a pencil line down the center, and if someone's elbow crosses it, that's an international incident.

But sharing erasers, borrowing sharpeners, and complaining about homework made the borders disappear. Somewhere between third-grade math and lunch, I realized... I had a crush on my desk partner.

It was completely innocent. No master plan. I was just happy that I got assigned to that desk.

Next year, nearing middle school, sections were getting reshuffled. A friend sat next to me and started drilling me: "Bro, who do you like? Tell me! I swear on my life I won't tell anyone."

I was stupid enough to believe him. I leaned in, whispered her name, and genuinely thought it was locked in a vault.

Within four minutes, he told every single friend in our group. And then he walked right up to the girl and told her too.

I was standing in the hallway, completely paralyzed. The whole group was laughing, she knew, and my soul basically left my body.

And then the cherry on top: another friend in our group ALSO liked her. So now two of us were publicly exposed and mourning our childhood romance together.

So yeah. That was sad. But at least I learned at age eight never to trust a friend with a secret.
