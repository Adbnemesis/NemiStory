Even after that legendary 'I know' confession, we didn't stop talking. We'd still banter in class, exchange homework notes, and occasionally text. Things were surprisingly normal.

Until one day... I saw her and her boyfriend get into this massive fight in the hallway. She was crying, visibly devastated, and my foolish savior complex immediately kicked into overdrive.

I felt terrible seeing her upset. So I grabbed my phone and texted her, badmouthing her boyfriend. Big mistake. She got furious, defended him, and BAM... I got blocked. Again. Second time in my life. That broke me, man.

Flash forward to the end of our second year. Out of nowhere, the news dropped: she and her boyfriend finally broke up. Five entire years together, officially over. Honestly, I felt bad for him too—he was actually a good friend of mine.

But the moment my other friends heard she was single? The squad went to work. Full-time matchmaking mode. They hyped me up, intervened on my behalf, and somehow... she actually unblocked me.

Summer ends. Senior year starts. I walk in thinking, 'Okay, maybe the universe is setting something up.' ...And then I find out: she's already dating someone else. Again.

And right there? A switch flipped. Instantly, all my feelings vanished. It was like the pedestal completely shattered. I saw her in a totally different way and thought, 'Yeah, nope. I do not like her at all anymore.'

With all that romantic nonsense out of the way, something unexpected happened. About five months before graduation, we naturally started talking again. No tension, no awkwardness, no hidden motives. Just genuine, normal friends.

We graduated on great terms. And when I went off to college? Well... that's where I met my actual girlfriend, Nemi. So in the end, it all worked out pretty nicely.
