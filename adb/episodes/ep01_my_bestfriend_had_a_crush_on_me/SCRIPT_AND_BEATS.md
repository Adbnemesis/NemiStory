# My Best Friend Had a Crush on Me

Script revision 1 • ADB • Target finished runtime: 90–120 seconds.

## Spoken script

My best friend once told me she had a crush on me.

And somehow, I was the last person in class to understand what was happening.

Back in school, we talked every day. Shared lunch, complained about teachers, borrowed homework. Normal best-friend stuff.

Then one day, a classmate pulled me aside and said, “Bro. She likes you.”

I said, “Yeah. We're friends.”

He said, “No. She LIKES you.”

Apparently, saying the same word louder was supposed to explain everything.

I laughed it off. These people couldn't even tell me the correct homework. Why would I trust their relationship advice?

But later, she came up to me. No smile. No laughing. Completely serious.

“Did he tell you?”

I said, “Tell me what?”

I knew exactly what. I just wanted another five seconds.

Then she said, “I actually like you. I've wanted to tell you for a while.”

And suddenly... I believed her.

Now I'm replaying every conversation we'd ever had. Wait. Was sharing her lunch a sign? Was borrowing my homework a sign?

Maybe I wasn't just useful at maths. Maybe I was... charming?

So I took a breath and said, “Okay, honestly, I—”

She burst out laughing.

“It's a prank!”

I looked over. The classmate was laughing too.

Right. Group project. Finally, one they'd actually contributed to.

I laughed along, obviously. Then spent the next period staring at the same page.

She asked, “You're not upset, right?”

“No. Just wondering how you remembered that whole speech but never remember your homework.”

She lost borrowing privileges.

For one day.

Then I needed her science notes.

## Direction brief

- **Promise:** Find out what happens when ADB's ordinary school friendship suddenly seems romantic.
- **Want:** Understand the confession without embarrassing himself or making the friendship awkward.
- **Choice:** Dismiss the rumor, then trust her serious delivery and start giving a sincere answer.
- **Consequence:** The prank reveal catches him just as he lets his guard down, exposing the classmate's setup.
- **Payoff:** His dignified homework sanction lasts only until he needs her notes.
