So after my friend broadcasted my primary school crush to the entire universe, middle school arrived. Our sections got reshuffled. Different classes, different wings, different desks.

The only thing we still shared was our second language elective: Japanese. So a couple of times a week, we were in the same room. Except in middle school, nobody talks. We went from sharing a desk to acting like strangers.

Still, in my head, I thought, 'We sat together all year. We're still friends.' So I messaged her on our chat app. Just a casual 'Hey, how's it going?' No reply. I waited days. So I foolishly texted twice: 'Did you see my message?'

Right after that, her profile vanished from my friends list. Completely gone. Any normal kid would know what happened. But my naive brain concluded: 'Oh! She deactivated her account! Respect to her for quitting social media.'

I lived in that peaceful delusion for a whole year. I genuinely thought she was just off the grid, studying hard.

Fast forward to our second year of middle school. In Japanese class, I overheard her giggling with her friends behind me about chatting late at night with her boyfriend on that exact app.

And the boyfriend... was the same guy from primary school who used to like her too. They were dating, texting all the time, and her account was very much alive.

My brain froze. If she's chatting every day... she never deactivated. WHICH MEANS SHE BLOCKED ME. She saw my two texts, felt second-hand embarrassment, and erased me from existence.

Bro, that honestly broke my twelve-year-old soul. I was so sad. I spent a whole year admiring a fake social media detox, when in reality, I was just blocked. Middle school is pure pain.
