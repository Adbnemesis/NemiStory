# ADB Episode 05: "My Crush Blocked Me" — Story Brief & Direction

## 1. Core Direction Brief (Five Sentences)

* **Promise**: A middle school kid thinks he's maintaining an innocent, quiet friendship with his primary school crush, only to uncover the most humiliating plot twist of his childhood.
* **Want**: Aiden wants to stay connected as friends despite their sections changing and middle school awkwardness driving them apart.
* **Choice**: He takes the brave step of sending her a casual text on their chat app—and when she doesn't answer, he doubles down and texts her a second time.
* **Consequence**: Her profile vanishes completely from his friends list, leading Aiden's naive brain to spend an entire year admiring her supposed "social media detox."
* **Payoff**: In second-year Japanese class, he overhears her laughing about texting her boyfriend—the same primary school rival—revealing she was never on a detox; she had ruthlessly blocked him.
