# Meet My Girlfriend

Script revision 1 • Animation direction revision 1 • ADB • Target runtime: 1.5–2 minutes (~110–115s).

ADB narrates the story of how he and his girlfriend Nemi met in college during Covid lockdown, how drunken honesty led to their first kiss, and how they built a 5-year relationship. All dialogue and quoted lines are spoken in ADB's canonical Aiden voice.

## Spoken script

People have been asking about the mysterious girl from my intro.

The one who shoved me onto the screen and made sure my character didn't look like a potato.

Well, today you finally get to meet her. Her name is Nemi.

And if you think we had some cinematic, romantic movie meet-cute... prepare to be deeply disappointed.

We met back in 2020. Middle of Covid.

College was basically twenty isolated students staring at black Zoom squares, pretending our audio wasn't working.

Nobody was making friends. Our daily social interaction was literally zero percent.

Until one Friday night, a small group of us in the same dorm block decided to have a small hangout.

Someone brought a plastic jug of neon mystery punch that tasted exclusively like hand sanitizer.

I was sitting in the corner with my laptop, minding my own business, trying to understand engineering thermodynamics.

Then Nemi walked over, looked at my screen, and said, “Are you seriously doing math at a party, or are you just pretending to look mysterious?”

I said, “Neither. I have a quiz on Monday, and I have zero survival instincts.”

She laughed, sat down next to me, and handed me a red plastic cup.

That was mistake number one.

Because one drink turned into three. Three drinks turned into a complete collapse of my calm composure.

By midnight, Nemi was violently debating animation frame rates, and I was passionately explaining table tennis spin physics like my degree depended on it.

Eventually, everyone else passed out. The noise died down, and we ended up sitting on the balcony floor talking until four in the morning.

We talked about college stress, childhood dreams, and how completely ridiculous the world felt right then.

And then... things happened.

We were drunk. We got completely honest.

I looked at her, she looked at me, and we kissed.

The next morning, we both woke up in a mild panic.

I cleared my throat and said, “Okay. We are mature adults. We will not make this weird.”

She said, “Agreed. Totally normal friends.”

That was five years ago.

We've been together ever since.

Five years of her stealing my hoodies, roasting my posture, and keeping me humble every single day.

So yeah. Meet my girlfriend. Best mistake I ever made.

## Delivery and runtime

Understated, witty, and dryly self-aware throughout the setup, shifting to genuine vulnerability during the balcony conversation and sunrise confession. The comedic spine is ADB's calculated engineering composure dissolving under cheap punch and genuine chemistry.

Suggested intentional holds:
- Beat after "Her name is Nemi" before dismissing the romantic movie fantasy.
- Comedic hold on "literally zero percent."
- Sharp deadpan pause after "That was mistake number one."
- Intimate, warm quiet beat after "and we kissed."
- Deadpan freeze on "Totally normal friends."
- Fond, settled closing smile on "Best mistake I ever made."

Target duration: 1.5–2 minutes (approx. 110–115 seconds). Preserves canonical Aiden vocal timbre at 1.00× pitch-preserving pace.

## Direction brief

- **Promise:** Finally reveal the true story behind the mystery silhouette from ADB's intro.
- **Want:** Survive college lockdown with dignity and pass thermodynamics without embarrassing himself.
- **Choice:** Accept Nemi's cheap party punch, drop his studious defense, and stay on the balcony talking till 4 AM.
- **Consequence:** A drunk, sincere confession and sunrise kiss completely shatters their planned "mature, totally normal friendship."
- **Payoff:** Five years later, the "mistake" is an enduring, loving partnership filled with mutual roasting and creative collaboration.
