# ADB EP03 — My School Found My YouTube Channel

Runtime choice: approximately 2–3 minutes; hard ceiling 180 seconds. Tighten prose if necessary rather than rushing Aiden. First delivery is the complete 1920×1080 review cut; current full-cut satisfaction is required before 4K.

Promise: The whole class discovers ADB's much older face-revealing channel during computer period.

Want: Share a small personal achievement with one friend without becoming the class entertainment.

Choice: Tell that friend about the channel.

Consequence: The friend opens the videos on a school computer and everyone watches; ADB feels exposed and embarrassed.

Payoff: Adult hindsight finds the memory sweet, recognizes the classmates' apparent admiration for roughly 1,000 subscribers, and returns to YouTube through an animated face.

One focus per thought. Held computer/phone art enters complete; live compact ADB marks reveal the private-to-public change and revise embarrassment into affection. Temporary reaction marks clear.
