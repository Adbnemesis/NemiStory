## 1. Character Acting Philosophy

> **COMPOSED CONFIDENCE WITH AN AUTHENTIC HUMAN HEART.**
> 
> ADB is not a cartoon caricature or an exaggerated anime stereotype. ADB's appeal lies in the tension between a **composed, stylish exterior** and **genuine, relatable emotional vulnerability**.

### Personality Contrast Principles
* **Calm Baseline**: When speaking, ADB moves with economy and intention. There is no frantic fidgeting, constant head-bobbing, or flailing arms.
* **The "Secretly Cute" Disarm**: When praised, flustered, or caught doing something embarrassing, ADB's cool posture cracks: soft blush floods the cheeks, the eyes widen slightly, and a sheepish half-smile breaks out.
* **Deadpan Delivery**: ADB is a master of the unimpressed reaction. When something ridiculous happens, ADB snaps into a stone-faced 0-velocity stare with horizontal dash lips and a raised eyebrow.
* **Passionate Sparkle**: When discussing a beloved topic (favorite anime, drawing technique, ridiculous tech quirk), ADB's calm demeanour dissolves into bright, enthusiastic energy with hand-flicked gestures and shining eyes.

## 3. Comedic Holds & Pacing

* **The Freeze Stamp**:
  - On strong punchlines or sudden absurd realizations, ADB instantly locks into a freeze.
  - Duration: `0.5s` to `1.2s`.
  - Zero tween movement, zero floating, zero mouth drift.
* **The Delayed Blink**:
  - After a comedic hold, break the freeze with a single, deliberate blink (`0.14s`) before resuming normal speech. This single micro-action immediately cues the audience that the narrator is back to reality.
* **The Slow Head Turn**:
  - To emphasize disbelief, turn the head slowly toward the camera over `0.45s` with deadpan eyes.

## 4. Emotional Escalation Progression

When building narrative tension across an episode:

```
Level 1: Calm Explanation  (Relaxed posture, gentle hand gestures, neutral eyes)
             │
             ▼
Level 2: Skeptical Intrigue (Head tilts, single eyebrow cocks, small smirk)
             │
             ▼
Level 3: Rising Frustration (Torso leans forward, sharp pointing gesture, narrowed eyes)
             │
             ▼
Level 4: Sudden Shock      (Snap backward, wide eyes, small 'o' mouth, brief trauma shake)
             │
             ▼
Level 5: Disarmed Cute     (Cheeks flush pink, sheepish smile, hand rubs back of neck)
             │
             ▼
Level 6: Absolute Deadpan  (Snap straight to camera, dash mouth, complete comedic hold)
```

## 5. Episode 00 Discovered Acting Techniques & Comedic Timing

During production of ADB's 0th intro episode (*"HI, I'M ADB."*), key reusable acting patterns were codified:

### A. The "Pushed Hook" & Silhouette Interplay
* **Physical Resistance vs Forward Slide**: ADB begins reluctant, leaning slightly away (`casual_slouch`, `look("away")`). When the mystery silhouette shoves from screen edge, ADB slides forward along the floor baseline without vertical floating.
* **Annoyed Glance Over Shoulder**: ADB halts, tilts head back toward the silhouette with furrowed brows, pauses, then sighs into the camera: *"Okay, okay, I'm here."*
* **The Second Outro Shove**: Used as the definitive episode cut button. During a polite sign-off (*"Thanks for watching, and I'll—"*), the silhouette swiftly steps in from behind and shoves. ADB stumbles forward, turns around with indignant wide eyes (*"Okay! LET ME DO THE INTRO!"*), cutting sharply to black.

### B. High-Contrast Athletic Stances vs Casual Drops
* **Anime Protagonist Stance**: When referencing serious athletic past (e.g. national-level table tennis), snap instantly from casual standing into `athletic_crouch` with `shouting` mouth, sparkling eyes, and radiating speedlines.
* **The Anti-Climactic Drop**: Immediately follow intense athletic action with an abrupt cut back to `relaxed_standing`, neutral eyes, and a relaxed paddle angle: *"Then I grew up... and retired my paddle."*

### C. Deadpan Delivery ("Great.")
* When faced with absurd creative or technical realization (e.g. thousands of animation frames on top of engineering work), hold hands static, shift expression into `deadpan` (`dash` mouth, flat brows), and deliver a monosyllabic line with zero extra motion. Follow with a `0.4s` silent hold.

### D. The Disarmed Sheepish Disclose
* When acknowledging behind-the-scenes assistance (*"...A LOT of help"*), trigger soft cheek blush (`set_blush(0.65)`), angle head slightly down-right toward the silhouette, and adopt a sheepish `shy_smile` before laughing it off with `pointing_up` at a comedic doodle (e.g. the potato).
