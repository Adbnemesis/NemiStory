# STORYTELLING RULES & CONVERSION MECHANICS

## 1. Core Principles
A successful carousel is an illustrated story, not an infographic slide deck.
Every slide must advance the emotional state of the reader:

1. **Stop**: Slide 1 creates instant recognition or curiosity.
2. **Lean In**: Slide 2 confirms they are in good company.
3. **Escalate**: Slides 3 & 4 heighten the stakes or comedic tension.
4. **Payoff**: Slide 5 delivers the punchline, twist, or insight.
5. **Save**: Slide 6 delivers a high-density, bookmarkable summary.
6. **Engage**: Slide 7 invites genuine discussion in comments.

---

## 2. Text Density Rules
* **Slide 1**: Max 12 words. High contrast, large font (60–72px).
* **Story/Body Slides**: Max 25 words per slide. Split into a 1–2 line headline and a short body punchline.
* **Takeaway Slide**: Max 35 words organized into 2–3 concise bullet points or a single framed quote card.
* **CTA Slide**: 1 punchy question (max 10 words) + character holding prompt sign.

---

## 3. Highlighting & Annotation Psychology
* Never highlight full sentences. Only highlight 1–3 punchy emotional words (e.g. `6 hours`, `12 views`, `completely broke`).
* Use handwritten annotations (`Caveat-Bold` or `GochiHand`) for inner-monologue reactions (e.g. `*pure pain*`, `*why did I do this*`).
* Highlighter marks should simulate soft watercolor washes (transparent peach or sage) positioned behind text layers.
