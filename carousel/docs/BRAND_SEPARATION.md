# BRAND SEPARATION & CREATOR IDENTITY COMPARISON

To prevent Nemi and ADB from blending into a single generic template, this guide documents how the two creator universes differ across every dimension:

| Creative Dimension | NEMI | ADB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Creator Archetype** | Passionate, self-taught animator; creative humility; relatable chaos | Relaxed, confident gamer/tech creator; dry wit; deadpan observer |
| **Humor Mechanism** | Self-deprecating panic, physical slapstick, dramatic hyperfocus | Deadpan timing, 0-motion freeze, sarcastic footnotes, unbothered smirk |
| **Topic: "Procrastination"** | *Over-animating a 1-second blade of grass for 4 hours while crying* | *Opening an RPG to "check daily rewards" and realizing it's 3:00 AM* |
| **Topic: "Burnout"** | *Sprawled on the floor hugging knees surrounded by crumpled sketches* | *Blankly staring into the monitor with a coffee cup, unblinking* |
| **Topic: "Algorithm Pain"** | *"I poured my soul into this!" (comedic dramatic despair)* | *"Ah yes, 12 views. Exactly as calculated." (deadpan realism)* |
| **Paper Canvas** | Warm Cream Paper (`#faf7f5`) with organic paper tooth | Warm Oatmeal Paper (`#faf6ee`) with technical dot-matrix accents |
| **Ink & Stroke** | Deep Blackberry Ink (`#38101e`) with calligraphic brush tooth | Deep Charcoal Ink (`#2b2623`) with clean architectural lines |
| **Props** | Drawing tablet & stylus, timeline scrubber, cat paws, sketchbook | Mechanical keyboard, gaming controller, smartphone, sleek mug |
| **Doodles** | Copper sparkles `✦`, pink hearts, panic hatch lines, sweat drops | Pixel stars `✦`, deadpan dots `...`, terminal brackets `>`, crosshairs |
| **Typography** | Expressive hand-lettering (`PatrickHand`) + handwritten margin notes | Structured, confident editorial headings + bracketed deadpan script |
