# ADB DOODLE GUIDE

Procedural decorative and technical micro-accents for ADB's universe:

1. **`pixel_stars` (`✦`)**: Crisp 4-point geometric glints in steel blue (`#4a5770`).
2. **`deadpan_dots` (`...`)**: Horizontal ellipses indicating speechlessness or comedic timing holds.
3. **`terminal_brackets` (`[ ]` / `>`)**: Minimalist code/terminal brackets framing footnotes or asides.
4. **`crosshair_reticle`**: Fine drafting lines with center crosshair indicating precision or gaming UI.
5. **`strikethrough_bar`**: Charcoal horizontal slash through crossed-out excuse or deadpan correction.
