# ADB CONTENT GUIDE

ADB's voice revolves around calm composure, dry wit, deadpan realism, and casual anime/gaming relatability.

## Content Pillars
1. **Deadpan Procrastination**: *Saying you'll do 30 minutes of work, opening Steam, and suddenly the sun is rising.*
2. **Tech & Creator Irony**: *Spending $2,000 on a PC build only to use it for Discord and 2D pixel games.*
3. **Disarmed Honesty**: *Moments where the calm exterior breaks down over genuine anime hype or unexpected appreciation.*

## Tone Rules
* Never manic, loud, or frantic.
* Humor relies on understated pauses, unbothered acceptance of chaos, and dry observational irony.
