# ADB POSE & COMPOSITION GUIDE

Poses are categorized by their spatial role in the composition:

1. **`peek_side_deadpan`**: Peeking around right border, horizontal brows, flat unblinking gaze directly at viewer.
2. **`lean_left_margin`**: Standing relaxed against left margin, one hand in pocket, other gesturing toward bullet list.
3. **`sit_card_crosslegged`**: Sitting casually atop text container with legs crossed, holding controller or coffee mug.
4. **`hero_bust_side_eye`**: Confident medium bust with subtle smirk and side-eye gaze toward the punchline.
5. **`disarmed_fluster`**: Sudden cheek wash blush, head tilted slightly away, hand rubbing nape of neck.
6. **`unimpressed_freeze`**: Grounded vertical posture, crossed arms, straight dash mouth, 0-velocity comedic hold.
7. **`sarcastic_shrug`**: Open palms out, head tilted to the side (*"who could have predicted this?"*).
8. **`hold_terminal_board`**: Holding a sleek modern card containing the concluding takeaway or question.
