# ADB CHARACTER GUIDE

## 1. Silhouette & Proportions
* Total Height: 6.2 Heads (~690px). Slender, relaxed, slightly taller frame (~1.20x relative to Nemi).
* Head & Jaw: Lean, softly angular jawline with refined chin contours.
* Eyes: Calm almond anime eyes with dark espresso irises and crisp white catchlights.
* Hair: Layered dark slate volume with middle-part curtain bangs framing forehead and cheekbones.
* Attire: Oatmeal knit half-zip sweater with ribbed collar/hem and metallic zipper pull, dark crewneck inner tee, straight-leg dark slate trousers, clean white canvas sneakers.

## 2. Character Integrity
ADB is an independent creator, not "male Nemi". His acting rests on calm composure, dry wit, and deadpan timing, with rare adorably disarmed moments when flustered.
