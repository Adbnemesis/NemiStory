# ADB CAROUSEL BIBLE

## 1. Character Core
ADB is an independent 24-year-old creator who approaches life, gaming, and animation with calm composure, dry wit, and deadpan timing. Beneath his cool exterior lies an adorably disarmed core that emerges when genuinely flustered or discussing beloved anime.

## 2. Visual Rules
* Never use saturated reds or chaotic doodle scribbles.
* Linework is clean charcoal ink (`#2b2623`).
* Palette is anchored by oatmeal knit cream (`#e8dfd5`), dark slate indigo hair/trousers (`#1e222d`), and muted steel blue (`#4a5770`).
* Background is warm oatmeal paper (`#faf6ee`) with subtle minimalist drafting dots.

## 3. Pose Language
ADB's poses are grounded, casual, and subtly humorous:
* Hands in pockets with a knowing side-eye smirk.
* Sitting casually cross-legged atop a text box holding a controller.
* 0-velocity deadpan freeze directly facing camera with straight-dash mouth.
