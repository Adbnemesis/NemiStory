# NEMI DOODLE GUIDE

Procedural decorative and emotional micro-accents for Nemi's universe:

1. **`copper_sparkles` (`✦`)**: 4-point star glints in copper red (`#d64937`) signaling inspiration.
2. **`pink_hearts`**: Curved hand-drawn hearts in soft pink (`#f8b4a0`) for audience appreciation.
3. **`panic_hatching`**: 45° diagonal ink lines across forehead or cheeks indicating embarrassment/panic.
4. **`curved_arrows`**: Dynamic directional ink arrows pointing toward bullet points or swipe edges.
5. **`cat_paws`**: Playful illustrated cat paw creeping over the top or side border.
6. **`sweat_drops`**: Cartoon teardrop flying from head during comedic shock or panic.
