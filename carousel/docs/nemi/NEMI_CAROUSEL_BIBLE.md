# NEMI CAROUSEL BIBLE

## 1. Character Core
Nemi is an authentic 24-year-old creative storyteller learning 2D animation. She is passionate, self-taught, observant, and subtly chaotic. Her humor stems from the brutal contrast between ambitious artistic vision and reality.

## 2. Visual Rules
* Never use cold grays or corporate blues.
* Never use generic vector icons or AI illustration tropes.
* The linework must have calligraphic tooth with blackberry ink (`#38101e`).
* Hair is always copper red (`#d64937`), hoodie is cozy sage green (`#76987f`), skirt is forest green (`#1d3d2c`).

## 3. Pose Language
Nemi's poses are physically expressive and comedic:
* Peeking nervously from corners when presenting an awkward truth.
* Clutching her tablet overhead in triumph when a 2-second animation works.
* Slumped across the bottom of the canvas when rendering fails.
