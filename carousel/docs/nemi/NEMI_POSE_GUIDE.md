# NEMI POSE & COMPOSITION GUIDE

Poses are categorized by their spatial role in the composition:

1. **`edge_peek_bottom`**: Lower torso cut off by canvas bottom, head and hands resting on edge, eyes tilted up at headline.
2. **`edge_peek_side`**: Peeking from right border, pointing with left hand toward the text block.
3. **`sit_on_card`**: Perched atop a framed text box with legs swinging, casual creative posture.
4. **`dramatic_panic_sprawl`**: Slumped across the lower third of the canvas, face planted or hands clutching head in creative exhaustion.
5. **`hero_sparkle_triumph`**: Medium bust, holding drawing tablet aloft with sparkling proud expression.
6. **`comic_recoil_shock`**: Upper torso recoiling backward at a 15° angle with sweat drops flying.
7. **`thinking_chin_tap`**: Three-quarters bust, tapping stylus against chin, gazing thoughtfully at headline.
8. **`hold_cta_sign`**: Holding an illustrated prompt board at bottom center prompting comments.
9. **`floor_knee_hug`**: Curled up in bottom corner hugging knees, staring into the creative void.
