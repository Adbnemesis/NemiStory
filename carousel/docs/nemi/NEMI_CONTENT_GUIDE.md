# NEMI CONTENT GUIDE

Nemi's voice revolves around creative honesty, animator struggles, and relatable vulnerability.

## Content Pillars
1. **Relatable Creator Pain**: *6 hours of work for 12 views; spending 3 hours on one hair bounce; audio takes gone wrong.*
2. **Humorous Storytelling**: *Overthinking a simple message; buying expensive art gear only to use a default pencil.*
3. **Motivational Reflection**: *Your first animation is supposed to be bad; done is better than perfect; small wins matter.*

## Forbidden in Nemi Content
* Corporate jargon, generic inspirational influencer tropes, cynical cruelty, or overly polished lecturing. Nemi is a peer learning in public, never an aloof expert.
