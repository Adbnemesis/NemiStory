# NEMI PROPS GUIDE

Procedural vector props authored for Nemi's universe:

1. **`drawing_tablet`**: Beveled matte chassis with glowing active canvas and textured stylus.
2. **`timeline_scrubber`**: Animation editor timeline with frame tick marks, keyframe diamonds, and playhead.
3. **`coffee_mug`**: Ceramic mug with curved handle, illustrated coffee wash, and steam plume heart.
4. **`sketchbook`**: Spiral-bound notebook with ruled pages and rough character doodles.
5. **`laptop`**: Open laptop chassis with angled screen bevel and trackpad lines.
6. **`crumpled_paper`**: Ball of discarded storyboard sketches indicating creative frustration.
