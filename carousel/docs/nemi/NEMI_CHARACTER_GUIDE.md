# NEMI CHARACTER GUIDE

## 1. Silhouette & Proportions
* Total Height: 5.2 Heads (~575px). Compact, approachable, expressive youth.
* Head: 94px width × 112px height with soft rounded cheeks (92px width).
* Eyes: Expressive emerald bean irises with dual non-mirrored white catchlights.
* Hair: Bouncing copper red hair dome with bangs fringe, cheek-framing side tresses, and ahoge bounce lock.
* Attire: Oversized dropped-shoulder sage green cowl hoodie, forest pleated skirt with knife pleats, white crew socks, chunky cream platform dad sneakers, cross-body black messenger bag.

## 2. Character Integrity
Nemi must never look generic, sexualized, or like a corporate mascot. She is a real 24-year-old animator with creative ambition, honest self-doubt, and sharp observational humor.
