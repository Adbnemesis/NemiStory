# CONTENT STRATEGY GUIDE

## 1. The 12-Stage Strategic Content Pipeline

Every carousel is engineered through 12 deliberate strategic steps:

```
[1. IDEA]                 Raw creative prompt or topic
     │
[2. AUDIENCE / ANGLE]     Identify target demographic & precise angle of entry
     │
[3. HOOK STRATEGY]        Pattern interrupt formula (Confession, Curiosity Gap, Contrarian Truth)
     │
[4. STORY STRUCTURE]      Narrative arc (Setup ➔ Escalation ➔ Crisis ➔ Punchline ➔ Takeaway ➔ CTA)
     │
[5. SLIDE PURPOSES]       Define explicit retention/conversion role for each individual slide
     │
[6. VISUAL TREATMENT]     Select brand aesthetic, paper tooth, linework weight, emotional palette
     │
[7. COMPOSITION CHOICE]   Dynamic per-slide archetype assignment (edge_peek, split_stage, card_perch, etc.)
     │
[8. CHARACTER & POSE]     Spatial composition pose matching the emotional beat
     │
[9. PROPS & DOODLES]      Storytelling props & recurring visual callback (e.g. coffee cooling)
     │
[10. TYPOGRAPHY]          Font pairing, line break balance, highlighter marker washes
     │
[11. GODOT RENDER]        Headless Metal execution producing 1080x1350 PNG slides
     │
[12. QA & VALIDATION]     Bounds audit, clipping check, brand isolation verification
```

---

## 2. Hook Generation Formulas (Slide 1)

Slide 1 must work as a standalone thumb-stopping thumbnail:
1. **The Relatable Confession**: *"Spent 6 hours on 3 seconds of animation."*
2. **The Curiosity Gap**: *"The tiny animation mistake that ruined my first 10 projects."*
3. **The Contrarian Truth**: *"You're not lazy. Your creative process is broken."*
4. **The POV Setup**: *"POV: You said you would finish the animation by midnight."*
5. **The Extreme Disconnect**: *"What I thought animating would be vs what actually happened."*

---

## 3. Curiosity Loops & Swipe Progression

To maximize carousel completion rates:
* **Trailing Connectors**: Slide bottoms feature subtle continuation cues (e.g., *"and then frame 14 broke..."*).
* **Directional Staging**: Character eye lines and pointing gestures anchor toward the right edge (leading the user to swipe).
* **Open Questions**: Slide 2 introduces an unanswered dilemma resolved only on Slide 4 or 5.

---

## 4. Narrative Pacing & Micro-Escalations

* **Slide 1**: Hook & Pattern Interrupt (Stops scroll).
* **Slide 2**: Baseline Setup (Grounds the relatable context).
* **Slide 3**: Micro-Escalation 1 (Effort increases or absurdity enters).
* **Slide 4**: Micro-Escalation 2 (Peak chaos, crisis, or realization).
* **Slide 5**: The Punchline / Reframe (Comedic payoff or emotional release).
* **Slide 6**: The Bookmarkable Takeaway (High-value, actionable advice formatted for saving).
* **Slide 7**: Low-Friction CTA (Relatable question driving comments).

---

## 5. Recurring Visual Callbacks

Every high-retention carousel includes a subtle micro-element that evolves across the sequence:
* **The Coffee Callback**: Slide 1 steaming hot full mug ➔ Slide 3 half-empty ➔ Slide 5 cold empty cup with skull steam.
* **The Timeline Scrubber**: Slide 1 clean 3-frame timeline ➔ Slide 4 exploded 500-layer chaos timeline.
* **The Battery/Clock**: Clock moving from 11:58 PM to 4:12 AM across the slides.
