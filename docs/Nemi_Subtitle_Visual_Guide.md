# NEMI — SUBTITLE & DIALOGUE VISUAL GUIDE (V1)
## Graphic Typography, Illustration Integration, Selective Emphasis, and Acting Choreography

---

## 1. Core Subtitle Philosophy: Part of the Illustration

In the Nemi storytelling framework, captions are **not** an afterthought or generic auto-generated accessibility text. They are an **active visual element of the hand-drawn illustration**.

```
GENERIC TIKTOK / SHORTS CAPTIONS            NEMI'S ILLUSTRATED GRAPHIC CAPTIONS
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐  ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ • Giant glowing center-screen text     │  │ • Clean, hand-lettered aesthetic       │
│ • Rapid every-word karaoke bouncing    │  │ • Natural sentence & phrase chunks     │
│ • Rainbow neon gradient fills          │  │ • Selective emphasis on key comic words│
│ • Obscures character's face & gestures │  │ • Sits elegantly in lower third        │
│ • Hyperactive, distracting visual noise│  │ • Syncs with acting beats & silences   │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘  └────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Three Golden Subtitle Principles
1. **Never Obscure the Actor**: Subtitles are anchored in the lower third or floating adjacent to Nemi's silhouette. They must never overlap her eyes, mouth, hands, or focal props.
2. **Support the Acting, Do Not Compete**: Subtitles appear *with* spoken syllable onset, but they clear or hold during deadpan silences so the viewer focuses on Nemi’s facial acting.
3. **Restrained, Hand-Drawn Typography**: The typeface must evoke clean modern comic lettering or warm humanist sans-serif with organic weight variation, rather than harsh machine fonts.

```
│                   ┌───────────────────────────────────────┐                 │
│                   │      "I had a completely foolproof    │  Y: 920px – 980px
│                   │        plan for this video."          │  Max 2 lines    │
│                   └───────────────────────────────────────┘                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Geometry & Constraints
* **Screen Position**: Centered horizontally (`X: 960px` in a 1920×1080 canvas), lower-third anchor (`Y: 920px – 980px`).
* **Maximum Line Length**: 42 characters per line.
* **Maximum Number of Lines**: 2 lines maximum per caption block (1 line preferred for snappy comedic beats).
* **Font Size**:
  * Standard spoken dialogue: `48px – 54px`.
  * Emphasized / punchline words: `56px – 62px` (+12% scale).
  * Whispered / embarrassed aside: `36px – 40px` (-25% scale).
* **Casing**: Sentence case with intentional lowercase for deadpan, embarrassed, or quiet moments:
  * *Standard*: `"So my plan was simple."`
  * *Quiet / Deadpan*: `"...it wasn't."`
  * *Emphatic / Shock*: `"WAIT."` or `"ABSOLUTELY NOT."`

## 3. Dual Art Mode Adaptation: Color vs. Monochrome

Subtitles must maintain perfect readability and aesthetic harmony across both supported render modes:

| Visual Attribute | COLOR CEL MODE | MONOCHROME INK MODE |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Text Color** | Deep charcoal brown (`#2B1E1E`) | Deep burgundy ink (`#3E081E`) |
| **Canvas Background** | Soft cool pale wash (`#EBF3F8`) | Warm creamy paper white (`#FAF7F5`) |
| **Emphasis Tint** | Warm coral-red (`#E05345`) | Rich crimson ink (`#753239`) |
| **Soft Whisper Tint** | Muted slate brown (`#706565`) | Soft plum ink (`#6E4A56`) |
| **Backdrop Outline / Glow** | Subtle semi-opaque paper-white rim (`#FFFFFFEE`, 3px soft stroke) | Zero harsh drop-shadow; clean crisp ink edge |
| **Illustration Tone** | Modern webcomic cel aesthetic | Traditional printed manga / indie comic page |

## 4. The Selective Emphasis System

Unlike karaoke captions that animate every syllable, Nemi subtitles use **selective emphasis** strictly to elevate punchlines, ironic stress, and comedic contrast.

```
"I told myself I was only going to watch ONE episode."
                                         ▲
                                         │
               ┌─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┐
               ▼                                                   ▼
       [METHOD A: WEIGHT SCALE]                            [METHOD B: COLOR ACCENT]
       • Font weight: Regular -> Bold                      • Tint: Neutral -> Warm Coral
       • Size: 52px -> 60px (+15%)                         • Optional hand-drawn underline
```

### Approved Emphasis Mechanisms
1. **Weight & Scale Shift**: The stressed word is rendered in a bolder weight and scaled up by 12–15%.
2. **Restrained Color Tint**: Emphasized words shift to the palette's primary accent color (warm coral `#E05345` in color mode; deep crimson `#753239` in monochrome mode).
3. **The Hand-Drawn Doodle Underline**: For major punchlines or deadpan revelations, a loose sketched underline or squiggly highlight appears underneath the word.
4. **The Bracketed Whispered Aside**: Embarrassed confessions or quiet realizations appear in smaller, grayed-out font enclosed in parentheses or asterisks:
   * `"I practiced for forty minutes. (With the window open.)"`

## 5. Subtitle Timing & Acting Choreography

Captions must never ruin comedic timing by displaying punchlines prematurely. Subtitle progression is strictly synchronized to **acting phases**:

### The Deadpan Subtitle Beat Choreography

```text
TIME    SPEECH                  NEMI ACTING                     SUBTITLE DISPLAY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0.0s    "I checked the audio."  Normal talking posture, eyes up  "I checked the audio."
1.2s    (Sentence ends)         Nemi stops speaking             Caption disappears
1.4s    [SILENCE]               Eyes snap to viewer, blinks once [NO SUBTITLE ON SCREEN]
2.4s    "...It was muted."      Flat deadpan stare, zero motion  "...It was muted."
3.8s    (Beat lands)            Subtle head shake               Caption fades out
```

### Timing Rules
1. **Sentence Boundary Respect**: Never lump a setup and a punchline into a single two-line subtitle block.
   * *BAD*:
     ```text
     "This was probably a good idea.
     It wasn't."
     ```
   * *APPROVED*:
     * Card 1: `"This was probably a good idea."`
     * *[Pause — screen clears — Nemi blinks]*
     * Card 2: `"...It wasn't."`
2. **Interruption / Self-Correction Handling**: Use em-dashes (`—`) to show sudden breaks in thought:
   * Card 1: `"I had everything planned out—"`
   * *[Cut to sudden slouch, pink blush]*
   * Card 2: `"Actually, no. I had a sticky note."`
3. **Punchline Linger & Clear**: After a punchline is delivered, hold the caption on screen for 0.8s to 1.2s to let the comedic beat register, then clear before the next thought begins.

## 6. Floating Graphic Annotations vs. Spoken Dialogue

In addition to spoken dialogue subtitles, the storytelling framework utilizes **hand-drawn graphic annotations** that pop up directly on the canvas:

```
                  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
                  │    TYPES OF ON-SCREEN TEXT   │
                  └──────────────┬───────────────┘
                                 │
         ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
         ▼                                               ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│       SPOKEN SUBTITLES          │     │       GRAPHIC ANNOTATIONS       │
├─────────────────────────────────┤     ├─────────────────────────────────┤
│ • What Nemi is saying           │     │ • Subtext, labels, timestamps   │
│ • Lower-third center            │     │ • Anchored near character/prop  │
│ • Clean, readable, sentence case│     │ • Hand-drawn, loose, comic font │
│ • Synchronized with vocal track │     │ • `*YAPPING*`, `<- My posture`  │
└─────────────────────────────────┘     └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Graphic Annotation Categories
* **Comic Sound & Mood Labels**: `*SHOCK*`, `*COPE*`, `*YAPPING*`, `*RAHHH!!!*` floating near Nemi's head.
* **Informational Callout Arrows**: Hand-drawn doodle arrows pointing to body parts or props (`<- Just woke up`, `<- Supposed to be a thumb`, `<- Never animated before.`).
* **UI Cards & Paper Notes**: Floating mock checklists, sticky notes, or subscriber counters crossing out old numbers.

## 7. Subtitle Implementation Checklist for Video Directors

Before rendering any scene in Godot:
1. Are subtitles positioned below the character's chin level (`Y >= 920px`)?
2. Does the text wrap at 42 characters or fewer per line?
3. Are punchlines separated from their setups by clean pauses?
4. Are all gendered self-descriptions absent (Rule 7)?
5. In monochrome mode, does text use `#3E081E` on creamy paper white?
6. Do captions disappear during deadpan stares to let facial acting breathe?
