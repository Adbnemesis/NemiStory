# Qwen3-TTS Official Voice Actors Audition — Nemi

Audition evaluation for official predefined voice actors in **Qwen3-TTS 1.7B CustomVoice** (`mlx-community/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice-bf16`).

Unlike VoiceDesign (which samples variable speakers from prompts), these official voice actors have permanent, fixed speaker embeddings embedded in the model weights. When an actor is selected, every dialogue segment is spoken by that exact same actor with **100% acoustic identity consistency**.

---

## Audition Script Lines
Each actor performed three distinct tones from Nemi's introduction script:
1. **Intro / Hook**: *"Wait, wait, wait—listen to me. Please stop scrolling for literally two seconds. Hi, I'm Nemi. I'm 24."* (Conversational, warm, friendly)
2. **Storytelling / Comedic Deadpan**: *"I spent four hours yesterday inbetweening an arm movement. It looked like they were slipping on three invisible banana peels... in slow motion."* (Casual, deadpan timing)
3. **Outro / Panic Stinger**: *"Thank you for watching my very first video. See you in the next one! Wait—did I leave the microphone gain at two hundred percent?!"* (Warm closing transitioning to panic)
