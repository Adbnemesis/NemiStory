# NEMI VOICE AUDITION SCORECARD & CANDIDATE EVALUATION

## 1. Audition Framework & Evaluation Criteria
To identify the authentic vocal identity of Nemi (a 24-year-old female animator and storyteller), 8 distinct candidate voice designs were synthesized using **Qwen3-TTS 1.7B VoiceDesign** running locally on Apple Silicon.

Every candidate was evaluated using:
1. **Identical Audition Script** (Section 27: 7 spoken chunks, ~62 words covering hook, self-introduction, comedic drop, and hobbies).
2. **Standardized Emotion Test Suite** (Sections 31–35: Deadpan, Excitement, Awkwardness, Shock).

### Evaluation Metrics (Scored 1 to 10)
1. **Human Naturalness**: Organic prosody, human-like cadence, natural phrase rhythm.
2. **AI Detectability**: Resistance to synthetic speech artifacts (10 = indistinguishable from a real human; 1 = obvious AI).
3. **Warmth**: Inviting, friendly, approachable vocal timbre.
4. **Pleasantness**: Believable feminine charm, vocal attractiveness without breathy exaggeration.
5. **Charisma**: Magnetic, engaging delivery that holds attention.
6. **Conversational Quality**: Casual, unscripted storytelling feel (sitting with a friend).
7. **Comedy Timing**: Ability to land jokes with natural comedic inflection.
8. **Deadpan Capability**: Ability to deliver dry, understated, deadpan lines without melodrama.
9. **Emotional Range**: Flexibility across casual, excited, awkward, and shocked beats without personality fracture.
10. **Storytelling Suitability**: Dynamic narrative flow for personal anecdote videos.
11. **Long-Listening Comfort**: Absence of vocal fatigue, ear-piercing sibilance, or grating mannerisms.
12. **Nemi Personality Fit**: Direct match to the 24-year-old creative, witty, self-aware animator persona.

## 3. Detailed Candidate Analysis & Human-Listening Tests

### Candidate 1: Warm Conversational (WINNER — PROVISIONAL CHOICE)
* **Prompt**: *"A natural-sounding young adult woman around 24, warm feminine voice, medium pitch, relaxed conversational speech, clear but not overly polished, subtle friendliness, naturally expressive, intelligent and approachable, slightly playful, sounds like a real person casually telling a story to a friend rather than reading a script."*
* **Human-Listening Evaluation**:
  * *Would I believe a real person could sound like this?* **YES**. The speech flow has organic micro-intonation without mechanical pitch stairstepping. It feels unscripted and casual.
  * *Could I listen to this voice for 2 minutes?* **YES**. The acoustic balance is soothing, crisp, and comfortable.
  * *Could I listen to it across 50 videos?* **YES**. The voice does not have any grating vocal quirks, artificial breathiness, or high-pitched fatigue factors. It establishes immediate intimacy.
* **Deadpan Test**: Understated, dry, and comedic without theatrical exaggeration (*"That seemed like a good idea. It wasn't."*).
* **Verdict**: **HIGHEST OVERALL FIT (113.4 / 120)**. Provisional voice selected for Nemi.
