# NEMI — ANIMATION & ACTING PERSONALITY GUIDE (V1)
## Translating Psychology into Rigged Motion, Facial Micro-Acting, and Comedic Timing

---

## 1. Core Acting Philosophy: Movement as Punctuation

In the illustrated YouTube storytime tradition, character motion is **expressive punctuation**, not continuous mechanical displacement.

```
GENERIC 3D / FLASH PUPPET                    NEMI'S ILLUSTRATED STORYTIME RIG
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐  ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ • Constant smooth 60fps limb tweening  │  │ • 85%+ static holds on crisp poses     │
│ • Floating, weightless rubber limbs    │  │ • 0-frame hard cuts on speech beats    │
│ • Expression morphs continually        │  │ • Sudden snap zooms (1.3x) on punchline│
│ • Feels like a corporate explainer     │  │ • Stepped 8-12fps loops for panic/rage │
│                                        │  │ • Living micro-acting: blinks & saccades│
└────────────────────────────────────────┘  └────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Three Golden Rules of Nemi Acting
1. **Pose-to-Pose Holds (The 85% Rule)**: Nemi snaps into a pose and holds it with rock-solid stillness while speaking. Secondary motion (ponytail settle, hoodie hem drift) settles within 3–4 frames.
2. **Saccadic Eye-Lead Preemption**: Nemi’s eyes always move 2 to 3 frames *before* her head or body turns. Humans look before they pivot.
3. **The Comedic Snap**: Transitions between emotional extremes do not ease slowly—they cut instantly on a single vocal syllable or punchline.

## 2. Complete 17-State Emotional Range Matrix

Every emotional state is calibrated for both **Normal Intensity** (everyday conversational beats) and **Extreme Intensity** (comedic climaxes and dramatic spikes):

---

### 1. Neutral
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Conversational baseline, casual observation, calm narrative exposition.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Relaxed emerald eyes, neutral eyebrow arch, gentle closed smile or slight talk mouth opening.
  * *Physical*: Upright posture, relaxed shoulders, hands casually resting near hips or waist.
  * *Timing*: Organic 2.5s procedural blinking, gentle 4-frame breathing oscillation.
* **Extreme Intensity (Comatose Neutral / Dissociated Blank)**:
  * *Facial*: Wide unblinking eyes with tiny pupils, completely straight horizontal flat mouth line.
  * *Physical*: Rigid vertical spine, arms hanging straight like lead weights.
  * *Timing*: 0-motion freeze for 3.0+ seconds; zero blinking.

---

### 2. Curious
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Asking questions, exploring an odd detail (*"Wait, why is that there?"*).
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Eyebrows raised slightly, eyes widened by 10%, mouth slightly open in an inquisitive curve.
  * *Physical*: Head tilted 8–10 degrees to one side, torso leaning forward 15 pixels toward camera.
  * *Timing*: Snappy 2-frame head tilt with a 3-frame damped overshoot.
* **Extreme Intensity (Forensic Hyperfocus)**:
  * *Facial*: Eyes narrowed to sharp slits with intense specular gleam, eyebrows furrowed in intense scrutiny.
  * *Physical*: Face thrust directly into the foreground (camera snap zoom 1.4x), holding a magnifying glass prop.
  * *Timing*: Sudden 0-frame snap into screen, holding completely motionless.

---

### 3. Happy
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Warm reflections, talking about favorite art, anime, or supportive comments.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Soft upward curved eye crescents (`^^`), cheerful open smile, faint coral cheek blush.
  * *Physical*: Relaxed open chest, head tilted gently upward, slight rhythmic shoulder sway.
  * *Timing*: Smooth 6-frame ease-in, gentle breathing cycle.
* **Extreme Intensity (Pure Euphoria)**:
  * *Facial*: Wide sparkling eyes with star highlights, broad open-mouth toothy grin, intense pink cheek blush.
  * *Physical*: Both hands raised in fists near shoulders, small rhythmic vertical bounce on 3s.
  * *Timing*: Stepped 3-frame bouncing loop held for 1.5 seconds.

---

### 4. Excited
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Talking about a breakthrough, discovering a new anime arc, mastering a lift.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Bright wide eyes, dynamic eyebrows angled upward, enthusiastic talking mouth shapes.
  * *Physical*: Gesturing with one hand raised, forward weight shift.
  * *Timing*: Fast brisk movements with crisp 2-frame stops.
* **Extreme Intensity (Uncontainable Fanatical Hype)**:
  * *Facial*: Giant sparkling eyes, floating golden sparkle doodles around hair, wide gasping laugh.
  * *Physical*: Both arms thrown high overhead, rapid stepped vibration, hair floating outward.
  * *Timing*: Rapid 2-frame stepped loop accompanied by slight screen shake.

---

### 5. Confused
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Encountering bizarre software errors, illogical instructions (*"Excuse me?"*).
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Asymmetric eyebrows (one raised high, one dropped low), gaze shifted upward-left, mouth pursed to side.
  * *Physical*: Head cocked 15 degrees, one hand raised scratching back of head, hip cocked.
  * *Timing*: Frozen hold for 1.8s while processing.
* **Extreme Intensity (Cognitive Blue-Screen)**:
  * *Facial*: Spiral eyes (`@_@`), wavy mouth line, three large floating question mark doodles.
  * *Physical*: Entire body tilted awkwardly off-axis like a tilted cardboard cutout.
  * *Timing*: Dead freeze with tiny 1-pixel horizontal twitch every 0.5s.

---

### 6. Suspicious
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Questioning someone's motives, sensing a trap in gym advice or tech claims.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Narrowed eyelids, tiny contracted pupils, tight horizontal mouth line, one eyebrow lowered.
  * *Physical*: Shoulders drawn in, torso leaning backward away from screen, side-eye gaze.
  * *Timing*: Slow, deliberate 12-frame creep into position.
* **Extreme Intensity (Sherlock Inquest)**:
  * *Facial*: Dark shadow cast across upper forehead, eyes glowing white through shadow, dramatic squint.
  * *Physical*: Collar of hoodie pulled up near chin, squinting through dramatic monochrome noir lighting.
  * *Timing*: Snap cut to noir monochrome palette, dead freeze.

---

### 7. Annoyed
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Dealing with slow export bars, tangled cords, minor daily irritations.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Downward-curved mouth, flat level eyebrows, sharp side-glance.
  * *Physical*: Arms folded across chest, weight resting on back foot, stiff neck.
  * *Timing*: Fast 2-frame snap into crossed arms, rigid hold.
* **Extreme Intensity (Boiling Irritation)**:
  * *Facial*: Twitching left eyebrow, grinding teeth mouth shape, red cross-vein doodle popping over temple.
  * *Physical*: Hunched shoulders, fingers tapping furiously against crossed arm.
  * *Timing*: Stepped tapping cycle at 12fps, sudden sharp glare at camera.

---

### 8. Frustrated
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Struggling with hands anatomy, repeated crashes, failed lift attempts.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Furrowed brow, tight grimace, gaze locked down on drawing tablet.
  * *Physical*: One hand covering face with fingers parted, shoulders slumped.
  * *Timing*: Slow 8-frame head drop into hand.
* **Extreme Intensity (Desperate Rage)**:
  * *Facial*: Wide open shouting mouth, dark cross-hatch shading covering eyes, steam puffs from ears.
  * *Physical*: Both hands tugging at hair or furiously waving a stylus in the air.
  * *Timing*: 4-frame screen shake impulse, violent ink scribble overlay.

---

### 9. Embarrassed
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Admitting a silly blunder, realizing someone witnessed an awkward moment.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Soft diagonal pink hatching across cheeks and nose, nervous wavy smile, gaze down-right.
  * *Physical*: Head ducked into hoodie collar, shoulders hunched forward, elbows tucked tight.
  * *Timing*: Fast snap into posture followed by an agonizingly slow 2.5s hold.
* **Extreme Intensity (Meltdown Mortification)**:
  * *Facial*: Entire face glowing crimson-pink, eyes squeezed shut into tight `> <` pinches, steam rising.
  * *Physical*: Hands clamped completely over face, entire upper torso collapsing onto desk or knees.
  * *Timing*: Instant drop downward, stepped trembling loop on 3s.

---

### 10. Angry
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Righteous indignation at bad design, unfair plot twists, or broken hardware.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Sharp angled V-eyebrows, open shouting mouth with visible upper teeth, blazing green eyes.
  * *Physical*: Forward lean, pointing single finger directly at offending object or camera.
  * *Timing*: Instant 0-frame impact on vocal stress.
* **Extreme Intensity (Volcanic Outburst)**:
  * *Facial*: Full Shonen rage face: flaming eye outlines, jagged jagged lightning background, roaring mouth.
  * *Physical*: Dynamic combat stance, hoodie flapping from invisible energy aura.
  * *Timing*: Full crimson background swap (`#753239`), 6-frame decaying screen shake.

---

### 11. Sad
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Mourning lost unsaved artwork, empathetic reaction to a tragic anime backstory.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Slanted eyebrows, downturned soft mouth, watery specular reflection in lower iris.
  * *Physical*: Defeated posture drop, arms hanging limp, chin tucked against chest.
  * *Timing*: Slow, heavy downward deceleration.
* **Extreme Intensity (Waterfall Despair)**:
  * *Facial*: Massive comedic anime twin tear fountains spraying horizontally from eyes, wailing open mouth.
  * *Physical*: Pounding fists rhythmically on desk or floor in cartoon agony.
  * *Timing*: Stepped 4-frame wailing cycle held on 2s.

---

### 12. Shocked
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Sudden unexpected realization, seeing subscriber jump, barbell slipping.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Pupils contracted to tiny dots, eyebrows shot upward into hairline, open circular gasp mouth.
  * *Physical*: Upper body recoils backward 30 pixels, arms splayed outward with spread fingers.
  * *Timing*: Instant 0-frame recoil, held rock-steady for 1.4 seconds.
* **Extreme Intensity (Earthquake Shock)**:
  * *Facial*: Eyes completely white circles, jaw dropped past hoodie collar, squiggly `SHOCK` doodles vibrating.
  * *Physical*: Body frozen mid-fall or mid-leap, camera snap punch-zoom 1.5x on eyes.
  * *Timing*: 1-frame snap with instant hard cut sound effect.

---

### 13. Terrified
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Facing stairs the morning after leg day, opening file explorer after corruption.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Blank pale eyes, vertical purple hatch lines falling down forehead, shivering lower lip.
  * *Physical*: Shivering spine, arms wrapped tightly around torso.
  * *Timing*: Cyclic 2-frame stepped shudder loop.
* **Extreme Intensity (Existential Horror)**:
  * *Facial*: Hyper-detailed realistic horror face (The Cursed Face / Wojak trope), dark ink splatters.
  * *Physical*: Complete petrification like a stone statue.
  * *Timing*: Instant 0-frame cut to external realistic drawing; absolute dead silence.

---

### 14. Smug
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Boasting about an airtight theory, predicting a plot twist, hitting a clean set.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Half-lidded eyelids, confident side-smirk, single raised eyebrow, optional glint on eye.
  * *Physical*: Chin raised high, chest puffed out, thumb pointing back to self.
  * *Timing*: Snappy 2-frame pose change with confident locked hold.
* **Extreme Intensity (Anime Glasses Glare)**:
  * *Facial*: Eyes completely obscured by blinding white reflective glasses glare, sinister grinning mouth, heavy cross-hatching.
  * *Physical*: Finger pushing bridge of glasses upward, aura lines radiating.
  * *Timing*: Dramatic snap zoom 1.4x into face, crimson or gold background accent.

---

### 15. Deadpan
* **Dialogue Behavior**: The ultimate Nemi weapon. Stating the plain, brutal, unvarnished truth.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Completely horizontal mouth line, eyes locked dead-center on camera lens, eyebrows perfectly flat.
  * *Physical*: Completely stationary silhouette. Zero head bob, zero shoulder sway.
  * *Timing*: 1.5 to 2.2 seconds of unbroken stillness. Only procedural blinks allowed.
* **Extreme Intensity (The Void Stare)**:
  * *Facial*: Same flat expression, but background drains instantly to pure monochrome paper white (`#FAF7F5`).
  * *Physical*: Rig motionless. Subtitles disappear completely.
  * *Timing*: 2.5 seconds of total dead air.

---

### 16. Relieved
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Finding an auto-backup file, surviving a workout, recovering from panic.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Eyes closed in soft happy curves, relaxed smile, giant blue anime sweatdrop popping off hair.
  * *Physical*: Heavy audible exhale drop: shoulders drop 20 pixels, head tilts back slightly.
  * *Timing*: Soft 10-frame ease-out downward into relaxation.
* **Extreme Intensity (Melting Puddle)**:
  * *Facial*: Chibi gremlin form melting into a literal puddle on the floor.
  * *Physical*: Proportions squash down to 30% height, floating soul doodle leaving mouth.
  * *Timing*: 4-frame stepped squash cycle.

---

### 17. Proud
* **Dialogue Behavior**: Presenting finished line art, showing off a completed video, conquering a challenge.
* **Normal Intensity**:
  * *Facial*: Sparkling wide eyes, warm beaming smile, rosy cheek blush.
  * *Physical*: Both hands on hips, chin held high, upright confident posture.
  * *Timing*: Crisp snap into pose with bouncy settle.
* **Extreme Intensity (Champion Fanfare)**:
  * *Facial*: Golden radiant sparkle aura, wide grin, sparkles bursting in eyes.
  * *Physical*: Holding up a sketchbook or tablet with both hands like a mythical trophy, golden rays radiating behind.
  * *Timing*: Rhythmic stepped bounce with celebratory doodle banner (`*I DID IT!*`).

## 3. Emotional Escalation Progressions

Nemi’s comedic power comes from **escalating transitions** where one state organically compounds into another:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[CURIOUS] -->|Notices odd flaw| B[SUSPICIOUS]
    B -->|Tries to verify| C[CONCERNED]
    C -->|Realizes catastrophe| D[REALIZATION]
    D -->|0-frame snap zoom| E[SHOCKED]
```

### Escalation Arc 1: The Detective Spiral
* `CURIOUS` (tilts head)  
  → `SUSPICIOUS` (narrows eyes, leans in)  
  → `CONCERNED` (eyebrows wobble, nervous smile)  
  → `REALIZATION` (pupils shrink, breath catches)  
  → `SHOCKED` (0-frame recoil, camera punch-zoom).

### Escalation Arc 2: The Hubris Collapse
* `CONFIDENT` (hands on hips, upright posture)  
  → `OVERCONFIDENT` (smug glasses glare, puffed chest)  
  → `SUDDEN FAILURE` (instant 0-frame frozen collision)  
  → `EMBARRASSED` (shoulders slump, cheeks flush pink)  
  → `DEADPAN RECOVERY` (blank stare into camera: *"...Yeah."*).

### Escalation Arc 3: The 3:00 AM Creative Crashout
* `HAPPY` (smiling, excited to draw)  
  → `FRUSTRATED` (eyebrow twitching at errant line)  
  → `ANNOYED` (arms crossed, glaring at canvas)  
  → `RAGE SCRIBBLING` (violent ink lines scratching across screen)  
  → `TERRIFIED HORROR` (cursed hyper-realistic face insert: *What have I created?*).

## 4. Physical Comedy Vocabulary

Nemi’s physical comedy is clean, readable, and silhouette-driven:

1. **The Recoil**: Rig snaps backward 35 pixels along the X/Y axis with splayed fingers on shock.
2. **The Slouch (The Shrimp Defeat)**: Upper spine bones drop 25 pixels downward in 1 frame, curling into a defeated posture.
3. **The Freeze**: Absolute cessation of all bone rotation and physics while dialogue or sound effects play.
4. **The Wojak / Hyper-Realistic Cut**: A 1-frame hard cut to a cursed, hyper-detailed pen sketch for shock contrast.
5. **The Stepped Flail Cycle**: A 3-frame looping run or panic cycle held on 3s (effective 10fps), expressing frantic distress.
6. **The Screen Shake Trauma**: A 4- to 6-frame decaying screen vibration on shouted syllables or heavy barbell drops.
7. **The Violent Scribble-Out**: Black or burgundy scribble strokes violently scratching across Nemi's face/body over 3 frames.

## 5. Subtle Recurring Mannerisms

These micro-acting behaviors make Nemi instantly recognizable:

* **Saccadic Preemption**: Eyes dart 2 frames before head turns.
* **The 10-Degree Tilt**: Inquisitive head tilt when processing odd information.
* **The Embarrassed Down-Right Gaze**: Eyes drop to lower right with soft pink cheek blush when admitting a flaw.
* **The Skeptical Double Blink**: Two quick 2-frame blinks when hearing something questionable.
* **The Pre-Confession Smile**: A tiny, sheepish corner-mouth smile appearing half a second *before* admitting a blunder.
* **The Camera Lock**: Freezing in place and staring directly into the viewer's eyes on deadpan beats.
