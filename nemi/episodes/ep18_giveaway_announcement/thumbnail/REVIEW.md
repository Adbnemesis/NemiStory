## Concept 1: "HALLOWEEN GIVEAWAY!" (Primary Candidate)

- **Headline**: `HALLOWEEN GIVEAWAY!`
- **Proposed Title**: `WE'RE HOSTING A HALLOWEEN GIVEAWAY! ($$$ PRIZES)`
- **Files**:
  - Native (1376×768 PNG): `thumbnail/revisions/concept1/thumbnail.png` (promoted to `thumbnail/thumbnail.png`)
  - Upload (1376×768 JPG): `thumbnail/revisions/concept1/thumbnail.jpg` (promoted to `thumbnail/thumbnail.jpg`)
  - Phone Review (320×180 PNG): `thumbnail/revisions/concept1/phone.png` (also at `thumbnail/review/phone.png`)
  - Tiny Review (160×90 PNG): `thumbnail/revisions/concept1/tiny.png` (also at `thumbnail/review/tiny.png`)

### Visual Findings
1. **Typography & Styling**: Broad, bold white sans-serif headline across the top with substantial nested BLACK / RED / BLACK contour outlines. Exactly matches the Raora editorial style. Fully legible at 160×90 mobile size without blurring.
2. **Character & Identity**: Nemi's canonical features are well preserved: warm orange-red hair with expressive ahoge, green eyes, green high-neck sweater, cheek blush, and warm burgundy outline aesthetics.
3. **Staging & Narrative Elements**: Nemi holds a cute carved glowing jack-o-lantern in one hand and points toward three prize display cards representing the custom PFP, drawing canvas, and shoutout. Cartoon bats add festive atmosphere.
4. **Composition & Margins**: Clean white background with subtle pale orange/cream checkerboard patterning and crisp white sticker-cutout edging with selective red outer glow. Lower-right corner has ample clear space for the platform video duration badge.
