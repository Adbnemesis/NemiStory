# Halloween Giveaway Announcement (R3 - Storytime Cut) — Nemi

A lively, hilarious, and engaging 35-second storytime announcement episode where Nemi announces her Halloween Community Giveaway for her Discord and YouTube audience.

---

## Spoken Script

**Line 0 (The Hook)**:
> "Stop scrolling, you guys! We are officially hosting a Halloween giveaway!"

**Line 1 (The Buildup & Drumroll)**:
> "And the prizes are amazing! Drumroll please!"

**Line 2 (The 3 Spooky Prizes)**:
> "Three prizes! A custom Halloween PFP, a personalized doodle drawn by me, and an official channel shoutout!"

**Line 3 (The 4 Requirements)**:
> "To enter: join our Discord, introduce yourself in introductions, subscribe on YouTube, and keep your DMs open!"

**Line 4 (The Rules & Shadow Clones)**:
> "Rules: one entry each! No shadow clones or burner alts, I will find you! And winners have 48 hours to claim."

**Line 5 (The Wrap Up & Good Luck)**:
> "No spamming, be nice, and good luck everyone! Happy Halloween!"

---

## Direction & Audience Focus

- **Promise**: An energetic, entertaining, and personal creator announcement that feels like a real community event, not a legal terms-of-service reading.
- **Want**: Reward true community members with personalized, hand-crafted artwork and channel recognition.
- **Choice**: Present the rules with comedic playfulness ("no shadow clones or burner alts, I will find you!") while keeping the guidelines completely clear and fair.
- **Consequence**: Excites the community, drives genuine Discord introduction activity, and maintains contest integrity.
- **Payoff**: A celebratory, warm, laugh-out-loud Halloween celebration leaving viewers hyped to participate.
