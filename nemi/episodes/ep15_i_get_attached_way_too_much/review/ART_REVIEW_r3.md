Inspected all six actual integrated R2 contact sheets (0.2–59.8 seconds), enlarged city-phone samples at 25.615, 37.480, 49.960 and 50.110 seconds, and actual R3 proof stills at 6.9 and 9.8 seconds. Immediately after the phone ends at the 50.1-second cut, the sampled sleeve/wrist returns to a non-gripping expression pose; no empty phone grip or leaked wrist angle is visible in the inspected following frames. The R3 closing proof places the simple phone on the actual drawing desk, removing the former floating desk vignette; the revised text sits above the tabletop. The 6.9-second text is mid-reveal, so that still alone is not an assessment of the finished lettering.
