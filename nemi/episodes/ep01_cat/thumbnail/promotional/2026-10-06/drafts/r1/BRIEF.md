## Variant A

Proposed title: **I Found a Kitten Nobody Wanted**

Single thumbnail phrase: **NOT LEAVING YOU**

An intimate giant perspective view of canonical Neeko in a newly authored damp box with Nemi's protective reaching palm. Editorial rain outside a warm oval of care is symbolic tenderness, not an invented storm incident. Kitten face is foremost, Nemi shares the gaze; rescue promise and no death cues.

The door is an invented visual barrier for the real refusal, not a literal film scene. Slate doorway dominates left and warm hopeful faces right; never imply Neeko died or Nemi permanently kept her.
