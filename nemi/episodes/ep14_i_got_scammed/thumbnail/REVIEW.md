## Mobile Readability Assessment

1. **Headline Contrast & Pop**:
   * Text: `"I Paid To Get Blocked!"`
   * Typography: Heavy white sans-serif with black-red-black multi-stroke contouring.
   * Legibility: Clear and readable at 160×90. The bold red mid-outline separates the white lettering against both the pale cyan checkerboard and dark drop shadows.

2. **Character Acting & Read**:
   * Nemi's facial expression conveys panic, blush embarrassment, and disbelief instantly.
   * White sticker outline separates her hair and green sweater clearly from the background pattern.

3. **Story Prop & Climax Hook**:
   * The phone screen with the prominent glowing red **USER BLOCKED** dialogue and exclamation marks clearly communicates the story premise at a glance without reading any subtitle text.
   * The sold-out concert tickets establish the music/concert setting.

4. **Timestamp Badge Safety**:
   * The bottom-right 20% area is intentionally kept clear of critical text or faces so YouTube's duration badge (`2:06`) will not obscure any key storytelling elements.
