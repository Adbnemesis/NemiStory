# I'm Embarrassed — Nemi EP16 Script and Measured Beats

Total Duration: 119.300s. Hard cap 125s. Canonical Sohee voice.

## Beat 1: 0.000–12.520s — The Dreaded Question
- **Script**: "Whenever someone asks what I do for work... my soul quietly leaves my body for about four seconds."
- **Thought**: The social horror of being asked a simple career question.
- **Focus**: Nemi's relatable mortification.
- **Visual**: Studio medium shot; Nemi starts explaining, glances away awkwardly, and shrinks as an off-screen speech bubble appears ("So... what do you do?").
- **Intent**: Hook the audience immediately with universal social dread.

## Beat 2: 12.520–27.768s — Normal Jobs vs. My Job
- **Script**: "Because normal people have real jobs. They do marketing, or finance, or they fix things. And then it's my turn, and I have to look an adult in the eyes and say... I draw cartoons on the internet."
- **Thought**: The ridiculous contrast between respectable careers and internet animation.
- **Focus**: Career comparison icons and Nemi's self-conscious confession.
- **Visual**: Metaphor cards of normal careers (briefcase, calculator, wrench) appear and clear; Nemi shifts weight and confesses with a soft shrug.
- **Intent**: Build the comedic disparity between societal expectations and independent creativity.

## Beat 3: 27.768–38.068s — The Polite Nod
- **Script**: "And immediately, they give you that polite little nod. The one that means: 'Oh... so you're unemployed.'"
- **Thought**: The unspoken translation of polite social pity.
- **Focus**: The awkward silence and deadpan reaction.
- **Visual**: Punch zoom toward Nemi's face; camera locks on her deadpan expression during the 1.5s pause.
- **Intent**: Land the first sharp comedic punchline through silent acting.

## Beat 4: 38.068–49.148s — On Paper, They're Right ($0.00)
- **Script**: "And the embarrassing part is... on paper, they're right. My total earnings this month? Zero dollars and zero cents."
- **Thought**: Admitting the brutal financial truth.
- **Focus**: Financial reality check.
- **Visual**: A drawn balance ledger showing "$0.00" in big hand-inked numbers; Nemi's shoulders slump in defeated honesty.
- **Intent**: Deliver total transparency without sugarcoating.

## Beat 5: 49.148–61.420s — The Empty Ko-fi Tip Jar
- **Script**: "I even set up a Ko-fi page. Just a little tip jar, in case anyone wanted to support my work. Total donations? Zero. Not even a single cup of tea."
- **Thought**: Putting herself out there and receiving total silence.
- **Focus**: The empty tip jar metaphor.
- **Visual**: A cute hand-drawn Ko-fi cup/jar with zero coins; Nemi gestures toward it and drops her hands into an embarrassed hold.
- **Intent**: Show the sting of unreciprocated effort with gentle humor.

## Beat 6: 61.420–76.546s — Invisible Labor
- **Script**: "And it hurts, because I'm not slacking off. I spend forty hours a week hunched over my tablet, animating frame by frame. But to the outside world, it just looks like I'm wasting my time."
- **Thought**: The huge gap between grueling behind-the-scenes effort and outside perception.
- **Focus**: The animation desk grind.
- **Visual**: Nemi at her drawing desk with tablet and stylus; timeline cards indicating hundreds of frames; transition to vulnerable confession.
- **Intent**: Validate the exhausting invisible labor of content creation.

## Beat 7: 76.546–92.731s — The Creeping Panic
- **Script**: "And what's scary is... late at night, I wonder if they're right. YouTube is getting stricter, views are low, and people move on so fast."
- **Thought**: The genuine fear of algorithmic obsolescence and lost audience attention.
- **Focus**: Vulnerable nighttime reflection.
- **Visual**: Soft cool evening wash; YouTube stats metaphor showing flatline reach; Nemi's gaze lowers in quiet doubt.
- **Intent**: Treat the fear of failure with real emotional respect.

## Beat 8: 92.731–103.171s — Existential Imposter Syndrome
- **Script**: "What if I pour years into this and never make it? What if this whole dream is just a very elaborate way to avoid growing up?"
- **Thought**: The deepest doubt: is this dedication, or just delusion?
- **Focus**: Intimate portrait of self-doubt.
- **Visual**: Closer portrait framing; Nemi looks down with subtle brow asymmetry and quiet stillness.
- **Intent**: Express the core fear every creative person secretly carries.

## Beat 9: 103.171–119.300s — Choosing to Draw Anyway
- **Script**: "I don't have a grand answer. I'm still terrified. But even with zero views and zero dollars... I still care about this. So I'm going to draw the next frame anyway."
- **Thought**: Reclaiming her purpose: love for the craft over external metrics.
- **Focus**: Quiet resolve and return to the tablet.
- **Visual**: Nemi lifts her head, a warm small smile emerges, camera widens back to the creator studio, and her hand picks up the stylus to draw the next frame. Still hold on the final frame.
- **Intent**: End with grounded dignity and determination, not a false corporate happy ending.
