# EP16 Thumbnail Candidate Review

Per user direction (*"also do the thumbnails as well, make them a little lewd check previous eps thumbnails"*), two distinct promotional concepts were generated using the approved image-generation workflow:

## Concept A: "DON'T LOOK..."
- **Headline**: `DON'T LOOK...`
- **Proposed Title**: *I'm Embarrassed by What I Do for a Living*
- **Composition**: Intimate close-up on the right. Adult Nemi is flustered and blushing intensely across her cheeks and ears, biting her lower lip while shyly pulling her oversized hoodie collar up to partially shield her mouth. Half-lidded glossy green eyes look up at the viewer with vulnerable/teasing appeal. A single floating red heart doodle near her hair.
- **Graphic Polish**: Massive heavy white sans headline with triple BLACK inner / RED middle / BLACK outer layered contours on pale cyan/grey checkerboard background. Crisp cutout sticker silhouette with red outer accent.

## Concept B: "JUST A TIP...?"
- **Headline**: `JUST A TIP...?`
- **Proposed Title**: *Hardly Anyone Even Donates...*
- **Composition**: Intimate close-up on the right, leaning forward toward the camera. Flustered, pleading expression with heavy rosy cheek blush, softly parted lips and a cute lower-lip bite. Both hands held close against her chest, shyly holding a small white ceramic coffee cup (Ko-fi tip jar with a heart and a gold coin inside). Red heart doodle floating near the mug.
- **Graphic Polish**: Bold double entendre headline `JUST A TIP...?` in massive white sans with triple BLACK/RED/BLACK outlines on pale checkerboard background. Crisp cutout silhouette.

## Mobile Legibility Inspection
- **320×180**: Both headlines are instantly punchy and readable at phone feed scale. Character expressions (eye contact, lip bite, blush) and props remain clear.
- **160×90**: Tested at tiny preview scale; high contrast outline ensures clean separation from the background.
