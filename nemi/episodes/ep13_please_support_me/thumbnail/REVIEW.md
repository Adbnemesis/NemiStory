# Visual Review — EP13 Thumbnails

## Inspection Summary
Both candidates were generated and reviewed at full resolution (1376×768), at phone scale (320×180), and at tiny feed preview scale (160×90).

---

### Concept A: "PLEASE SUPPORT ME!" (Staged Candidate)
- **Visuals**: Nemi is front-and-center, looking directly at the viewer with hands clasped in front of her chest in a sweet, pleading, sincere gesture. On the left is her drawing tablet showing '3:00 AM' and a cute ceramic coffee mug with a coin tip heart.
- **Typography**: Single headline `PLEASE SUPPORT ME!` rendered in massive broad rounded white sans with thick black inner / red middle / black outer triple contours. Text remains 100% legible even at 160×90.
- **Background & Edging**: Clean white background with pale cyan checkerboard and sparse ink doodles (Discord icon, pencil, notepad, coin). Crisp white sticker cutout outline with selective red outer glow.
- **Character Continuity**: Faithful to Nemi's canonical appearance (red-orange hair, long ahoge, large green eyes, blush, dark green high-neck sweater, plum ink).

---

### Concept B: "JOIN MY CORNER!" (Alternative Candidate)
- **Visuals**: Nemi on the right, large and energetic, smiling playfully while holding up a video game controller and pointing at the viewer. On the left is a Discord card badge labeled "Nemi's Corner" with cat ears and a coffee cup with a tip coin.
- **Typography**: Single headline `JOIN MY CORNER!` with identical thick black/red/black triple contour treatment.
- **Background & Edging**: Clean white with pale cyan / lavender checkerboard pattern, game sparkles, and cutout borders.

---

## Mobile & Feed Legibility Review
- **Phone scale (320×180)**: Both headlines are immediately readable in <1 second; Nemi's facial expressions and eye contact remain distinct.
- **Tiny search feed (160×90)**: The high contrast black/red/black outlines ensure text does not blur into the background. Character silhouette is instantly recognizable.
