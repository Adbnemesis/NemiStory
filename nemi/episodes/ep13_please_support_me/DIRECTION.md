# EP13 Direction Brief: Please Support Me (R3 Cut)

## Core Direction & Narrative Structure
- **Promise:** Deliver a brisk, candid 1-minute creator conversation that eliminates dragged-out speech, introduces Discord games, and directly emphasizes financial support on Ko-fi.
- **Want:** Upgrade her animation device, support her family, become financially independent doing what she loves, and build a lively Discord community with games and art.
- **Choice:** Open up "Nemi's Little Corner" for games, art, and chatting, while providing a clear Ko-fi link for viewers wishing to support her financially.
- **Consequence:** Heartfelt vulnerability—sharing the real reasons financial support matters (better device, supporting family, gaining independence) with zero-pressure gratitude.
- **Payoff:** An authentic, warm, and inspiring message that leaves the audience knowing exactly how to connect and support her.

## Visual Language & Balance (EP11 R10 Upper-Middle Standard)
- **Palette & Setting:** References the EP06 "How I Animate" paper baseline. Studio desk setting with warm cream paper (`#F9F6EE`), dark plum character ink (`#4A2E35`), and soft watercolor accents.
- **Acting & Pacing:** Fast, purposeful emotional transitions. Direct candid gaze on the hook, snappy 3 AM grind (finishing by 9.5s), bashful downward glance on loneliness, bright energized smile on the Discord reveal, and warm grateful hand-to-heart on zero pressure and family support.
- **Drawings & Props:**
  - Physical props: Graphic tablet, laptop, ceramic coffee mug, draft sketch pages, viewer comment card.
  - Live doodles: "straight to the point", "3:00 AM", scratch scribble, "NEMI'S CORNER", sparkle accents, "games & art", "SUPPORT ME", "thank you! <3", "better device", "family & independent", "links below!", and "see you soon!".
  - Completed ink settles immediately; no drifting, jitter, or artificial floating motion.

8. **Shot 7 (Device, Family & Independence):** Medium shot with gentle camera punch, sincere animator resolve.
9. **Shot 8 (Outro Wave):** Cheerful portrait framing as Nemi points down and waves goodbye.
