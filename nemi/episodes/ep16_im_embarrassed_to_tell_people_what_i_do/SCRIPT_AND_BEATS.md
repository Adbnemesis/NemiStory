# EP16 — measured script and thought beats

Runtime 107.400s, hard cap120s. Fictionalized dramatization. Canonical Sohee.

## 1. 0.000–9.418s

So, what do you do? I was at a cafe. Normal question. Apparently, I needed a lawyer.

Focus: Nemi’s face then cafe geography. Cafe wide and quiet portrait, no literal lawyer art. Make the social tension immediate.

## 2. 9.418–16.788s

I said, I work on a computer. So does the person fixing the card machine. We had narrowed it down to electricity.

Focus: Evasive label then dry face. Computer work clears; cafe stays ordinary. Let the electricity line land.

## 3. 16.788–28.624s

They asked what kind of work. I said, creative stuff. Creative stuff? Excellent. Now I sounded like I sold suspicious candles.

Focus: One handwritten creative label. Write, question and cross out only the current answer. The attempted escape causes the next awkward thought.

## 4. 28.624–38.928s

I could've just said animator. At home, I'll explain a three-frame blink like I'm defending a thesis. In public? Computer activities.

Focus: Creator desk then social freeze. Story pages and brief underline contrast with a deadpan return. Keep Nemi capable rather than helpless.

## 5. 38.928–45.918s

Then they asked to see something. My phone suddenly felt like it contained every bad hand I'd ever drawn.

Focus: Held phone and worried face. Already-held phone has continuous supported grip. Make the stakes concrete.

## 6. 45.918–54.492s

I opened a video. Then immediately started explaining why the old drawings didn't count. The hair was wrong. The timing was weird.

Focus: Video evidence then draft pages. One upload card clears before two old drafts. The evidence contradicts the need to apologize.

## 7. 54.492–59.712s

I was giving a guided tour of the emergency exits before they'd even watched it.

Focus: Dry face. No extra illustration, sound or movement. Earn a clean comedic hold.

## 8. 59.712–68.192s

They laughed at the little joke. Not at me. At the part I'd spent an entire evening trying to make funny.

Focus: Listening eyes then relief. Quiet recognition and held story page at the studio. Let work connect with a person.

## 9. 68.192–78.332s

Then came the terrifying follow-up. You made that? I almost said, sort of. As if the drawings had wandered in by themselves.

Focus: Face and short sort-of label. The label clears before the honest answer. Prepare the choice without another panic burst.

## 10. 78.332–85.152s

But I said, yeah. I make animated stories. My voice did that tiny wobble. The cafe continued operating.

Focus: Honest face then ordinary cafe. Short animated stories lettering settles, then wide quiet room. Answer the opening question.

## 11. 85.152–92.732s

Nobody asked for a certificate. They just wanted to see the next bit. I was the only one making me audition.

Focus: Story page then reflective face. Turn one page and hold the admission. Keep the judgment inside Nemi rather than inventing an adversary.

## 12. 92.732–107.400s

So, next time someone asks, I'm an animator. Still learning. Still redrawing hands. Apparently, I'm allowed to say it before I'm finished.

Focus: Full body then final face. One word and notebook callback clear for the quiet ending. End on small earned pride, not a lecture.
