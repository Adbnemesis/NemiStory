# EP10 R5 palette reference and application

Reference: Nemi EP06 How I Actually Make Storytime Animations, original 1080p film. Inspected real frames at 2, 12, 27, 42, 60, 78, 96 and 115 seconds. Read the colour constants and selected studio implementation in Ep06Backdrop.gd; the full historical player was not reused.

Observed cream #faf7f2 canvas, warm #f2ebe0 floor, #2e1822/#6b5763 contour hierarchy, canonical full-colour Nemi, pale blue windows/grids and selective small coloured props/annotations. Its dark computer screen is a bounded object, not a full-room mood fill.

Application: school/street/bedroom/office remain actual illustrated locations, with paper walls and warm neutral floors. Keep the richer furniture, books, shop display, plants, doors and event-specific diagrams. Remove broad navy/peach/mint walls and coloured facades; use pale window washes and amber only for the light/lamp clue. Curtains and gate become neutral paper fills. Paper diagrams have light surfaces; rose flags secrecy, teal differentiates routes/evidence. Nemi's canonical auburn/sage colours stay untouched.

Retain R4's live ink, thought-led poses, camera paths and stronger sound; palette revision does not restore the rejected sparse R3 staging. Wrist restoration outside bounded phone ownership remains active. Narration and measured word times stay unchanged at 137 seconds. Full review is 1080p only; no 4K approval is recorded.

The reference is a colour-treatment model, not permission to copy EP06 sets, private players, procedural timers, historical voice rules or its script. Thumbnail palettes remain independent.
