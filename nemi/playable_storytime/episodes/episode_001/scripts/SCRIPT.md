# I Volunteered for Free Pudding. You Have to Help.

An original fictional story performed by Nemi.

## 0:00 — PRACTICE · PRESS 1

Look, I saw free pudding on a school volunteer form, and I signed before I reached the part explaining what I'd volunteered for. You're taking over before I make it worse. Drag to look around and press one to start. On phones, swipe and use numbered chapters. If you're paused, press play.

## 0:24 — 1 · BADGE OR LUNCH

My badge is somewhere on the left, next to a sandwich that's honestly looking more useful than I am. The badge gets us through that door; the sandwich lets me avoid making a decision for another five minutes. Three for the badge, two for lunch. Please don't encourage me. I'm very easy to encourage.

## 0:48 — 2 · SANDWICH ROUTE

Okay, let's hear the sandwich out. It's got layers, it's well prepared, and it hasn't agreed to do anything embarrassing today. Honestly, it should be wearing my badge. Unfortunately, I can't send bread through that door and go home, so three gets the badge. We'll keep the sandwich as an emergency understudy.

## 1:12 — 3 · BADGE FOUND

Oh, thank goodness, there's my badge. Hang on, why does it say HOST? I thought I'd signed up to help, you know, carry a box, point at a door, quietly disappear when somebody mentions a microphone. Apparently I ticked the enthusiastic box. There's an enthusiastic box? Four, please. We need the noticeboard.

## 1:36 — 4 · WHICH ROOM?

Right, the noticeboard. The sensible new sign says assembly, and the old one says pudding. Before you say anything, I can see that it's crossed out. I'm choosing to interpret that as decorative. Six gets us to assembly; five lets us investigate the pudding. You can probably guess which one I'm hoping for.

## 2:00 — 5 · PUDDING ROUTE

There's a cup! See, I told you this was worth investigating. Oh, those are pencils. I've come to the stationery cupboard for pudding, and somehow I'm still looking for a spoon. Please don't tell the sandwich; it'll think it's won. Come on, six gets us to assembly before I start checking glue sticks for ingredients.

## 2:24 — 6 · ASSEMBLY REACHED

That's the microphone. I can explain an entire anime power system to somebody who didn't ask, but apparently good morning requires rehearsing until my personality leaves my body. Before I get up there, could you check who's watching? Seven opens the last choice. I'm going to practise looking like this was my idea.

## 2:48 — 7 · HIDE OR HOST?

Hang on, turn around for a second. They're toys. It's a rehearsal! I've spent the entire morning rehearsing my apology to a duck. And look at its face, why does it seem like it knows? Nine takes the microphone; eight lets me hide. Please pick before I start explaining myself to the bear.

## 3:12 — 8 · HIDING ROUTE

Okay, new plan: I stand completely still and become school furniture. Nobody expects a chair to introduce the assembly. Why am I making eye contact with the duck? It's a toy, Nemi, it doesn't know anything. Nine, please. I'd rather do the speech than lose a staring contest to stuffing.

## 3:36 — 9 · ON STAGE

Good morning, everyone, and welcome to whatever this is. Right, nobody fainted. The duck looks exactly as disappointed as before, so I'm calling that progress. Thank you for staying with me. And the pudding was last Friday. Of course it was. Zero if you'd like to try talking me out of this next time.
