# R9 — Three choices, one practice

**Look. Choose a named action. Press its number.**

Start with the practice: focus the desktop video picture, then press **1** to open the badge round. On a phone, use the YouTube app and select the chapter beginning **1**. Drag or swipe to look around. If you paused, K / Play continues after a choice.

Only the current options matter:

| Round | Option A | Option B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Badge or lunch | **2 — Inspect sandwich** | **3 — Use star badge** |
| Which room? | **5 — Try pudding room** | **6 — Go to assembly** |
| Hide or host? | **8 — Hide in plain sight** | **9 — Take the microphone** |

Every arrival repeats its number and result. Comic routes show one number to continue; there is no inventory to remember. Keep watching if you prefer the whole story without input. At the ending, **0** plays again.

| Device | Look | Choose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop YouTube | Drag, or use WASD with the player focused | Press the displayed top-row number once. K / Play resumes if paused. |
| YouTube phone/tablet app | Swipe | Open the numbered chapter from the player chapter list or description. |
| Local review page | Drag/swipe or focused WASD | Use its current-choice controls. Any buttons are local review conveniences. |

The full video must stay 240.000 seconds: YouTube number keys jump to percentages, with each numbered section 24 seconds apart. The movie cannot receive clicks on a drawn object or detect whether you solved a puzzle. Captions are conventional dialogue text.

The 1920×1080 director review is a flat movie. Use the tagged 2160×1080 sphere in the local game viewer or a supported YouTube 360 player to look around. View direction stays where you left it, so the result cards repeat across the four directions.
