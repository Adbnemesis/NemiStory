HOW TO PLAY
Start with the short practice. On desktop, click the video picture and press 1 to begin. Drag or use WASD to look around. At each choice, press one displayed top-row number. If paused, K / Play continues.

On a phone or tablet, open the YouTube app, swipe to look around, then select the matching numbered chapter from the player chapter list or description. Numbers drawn in the movie are instructions, not touch buttons.

Only the current options matter. Each arrival repeats its number and result and gives one simple way forward. Keep watching if you'd rather enjoy the whole story. At the ending, 0 plays again.
