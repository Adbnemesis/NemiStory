## Apply the theme

Keep paper continuity across scene changes. Establish actual locations with inked furniture, windows, doors, street geometry and story-specific objects; cream space must not become an excuse for an empty generic stage. Build depth through contour hierarchy, a few soft shadows/washes, layering and grounding.

Simple backgrounds remain recognizable and visible. The user approved Nemi EP11 R10's **upper-middle balance**: restrained studio scenery with sustained poses/expressions, props, held/live doodles, camera changes and event-linked sound developing sequentially around one audience focus. Reducing clutter changes simultaneous density, scale or duration; it does not erase scenery or the performance. Adding energy develops the thought; it does not accumulate every prop/mark/effect permanently. Let completed ink rest, clear temporary accents after their purpose and leave room for emotional stillness. See the [accepted direction example](../../nemi/episodes/ep11_i_really_need_your_help/scene_r10_1080p.json) and [refinement guidance](STORYTIME_REFINEMENT_WORKFLOW.md). The example's counts and specific studio layout are not quotas or defaults for other stories.

Preserve each character's existing colour-mode design and author-specific pen geometry. Use colour on the current focal object or meaningful annotation: for EP10, a lit window/lamp may carry amber, a warning/secret may carry rose, and a route or corroborated evidence may carry teal. Those are this episode's choices, not an obligatory channel palette or a promise that every prop needs a different hue. Neutral supporting scenery keeps attention on the character and evolving evidence.

For evening/tension, use a restrained cool/grey paper wash, shaded window insert or ink contrast while preserving the light overall canvas. A deliberately dark bounded object can serve the story; broad navy walls, saturated roads and alternating multicolour building blocks are not the default. Avoid grey low-contrast ink as the only remedy for excessive colour. Keep the drawn contours and key marks legible.

Match the **colour treatment**, not the literal studio setting. A school story still needs a classroom, street/shop, bedroom and office. Keep current operational timing, grounded contact, stable completed ink, finite effects and voice rules; do not import EP06's historical procedural timers/private players or replace the current version-2 stage.
