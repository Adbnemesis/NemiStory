# VOICEOVER & AUDIO SPECIFICATIONS
## Directory Guidelines

Place audio assets for this episode in this folder:

* **`voiceover.wav`**: Master dialogue recording.
  * Format: Uncompressed PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz or 48.0 kHz, 16-bit or 24-bit, Mono or Stereo.
  * Audio level: Normalized to -14 LUFS, peaks capped at -1.0 dB.
* **`sfx/`**: Episode-specific sound effects (e.g. `whoosh.wav`, `pencil_scratch.wav`, `ding.wav`).
* **`music/`**: Background music tracks (e.g. `lofi_beat.mp3`, `acoustic_guitar.wav`). Should be mixed -18dB to -24dB relative to dialogue.
