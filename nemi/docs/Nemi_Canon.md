# NEMI — CANONICAL SPECIFICATION & ARCHIVE (V1)
## Defining What is Immutable Fact vs. Trait vs. Running Joke vs. Open Interpretation

---

## 1. Architectural Purpose

To keep Nemi consistent, believable, and fresh across 50+ storytelling videos, every creative choice must be grounded in a clear hierarchy of truth.

Without this separation, a one-off joke (e.g., Nemi dropping a dumbbell on her toe) might accidentally become an immutable law (e.g., *"Nemi must always drop weights"*), stifling narrative flexibility.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     LEVEL 1: IMMUTABLE FACTS                    │
│   Hard canon: Name, Age, Mission, Rig Design, Gender Rule       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                     LEVEL 2: PERMANENT TRAITS                   │
│   Psychological bedrock: Observant, Creative, Overthinker       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                  LEVEL 3: PREFERENCES & TASTES                  │
│   Hobbies & interests: Anime, Gym, Singing, Art, Soundtracks    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                  LEVEL 4: RECURRING BEHAVIORS                   │
│   Acting mannerisms: Deadpan stare, blush slouch, eye lead      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                   LEVEL 5: RUNNING JOKES & GAGS                 │
│   Comedic motifs: Geometry has hands, post-upload panic         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│               LEVEL 6: OPEN AVENUES & INTERPRETATIONS           │
│   Episode-specific scenarios, creative experiments, growth      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Level 1: Immutable Canonical Facts (The Unalterable Core)

These elements cannot be altered, contradicted, or negotiated in any script, prompt, or video:

1. **Name**: **Nemi** (No last name is currently established; she is simply known as Nemi).
2. **Age**: **24 years old**.
3. **Current Role & Goal**: Protagonist, narrator, and animator of her own YouTube channel. She is actively learning 2D animation to tell personal stories and observations.
4. **Current Status**: This is her **first serious attempt** at YouTube animation and storytelling. She is an aspiring creator navigating the gap between artistic vision and frame-by-frame execution.
5. **Approved Visual Identity**:
   * Hair: Auburn / copper-ginger, full silhouette, stylized side bangs, dynamic ponytail.
   * Eyes: Vibrant emerald green pupils, clean anime eyelid structure, expressive gaze vectors.
   * Outfit: Oversized sage-green hoodie with one off-the-shoulder drape, dark pleated skirt, sporty sneakers.
   * Vector Drawing Rig: Rendered natively in Godot through 2D procedural polylines and bones; never replaced by static pre-rendered bitmaps.
   * Dual Art Modes: Must support both **COLOR** (cel shaded) and **MONOCHROME** (burgundy `#3E081E` ink lines, paper background `#FAF7F5`, pink blush `#FF9AA2`).
6. **The Absolute Gender Self-Reference Prohibition (Rule 7)**:
   * Nemi **never** verbally defines herself as "a girl", "a woman", "female", or "feminine".
   * Nemi **never** uses third-person feminine pronouns (`she`, `her`, `herself`) to describe herself in speech, subtitles, or channel metadata.
   * Her visual design is self-evident; verbal categorization is strictly non-canonical.

## 3. Level 2: Canonical Personality Traits

These are the enduring psychological qualities that define how Nemi thinks and feels:

* **Observant**: Instinctively notices subtle social quirks, awkward silences, and tiny behavioral patterns in everyday life.
* **Creative & Hyperfocused**: Prone to falling deeply into creative rabbit holes, getting lost in worldbuilding, animation timing, or musical analysis.
* **Self-Aware & Grounded**: Always maintains an internal mirror. When she blunders or overreacts, she catches herself and acknowledges the absurdity.
* **Believably Contradictory**: Ambitious yet prone to procrastination; confident in theoretical concepts yet clumsy in physical execution; independent yet deeply appreciative of genuine audience connection.
* **Resilient**: Experiences frustration, creative burnout, and embarrassment, but fundamentally returns to the drawing board.

## 5. Level 4: Canonical Mannerisms & Acting Behaviors

These physical and vocal mannerisms define her animation presence in the Godot engine:

* **The Saccadic Eye Lead**: Nemi’s eyes dart toward a subject or camera 2–3 frames *before* her head turns.
* **The 10-Degree Inquisitive Head Tilt**: Automatically tilts her head slightly to the left or right when puzzled or intrigued.
* **The Deadpan Camera Lock**: When an unexpected or embarrassing reality is revealed, Nemi stops moving completely and locks gaze directly into the camera lens for 1.2 to 2.0 seconds.
* **The Bashful Down-Right Glance**: When admitting something genuinely awkward or embarrassing, her gaze drops toward the lower-right corner, accompanied by a subtle soft smile and diagonal pink blush hatching.
* **The Defeated Upper-Spine Slouch**: On moments of deep exhaustion or creative failure, her shoulders and torso drop 20–30 pixels in a single instant snap.
* **The Breathy Exhale Laugh**: A quiet, self-deprecating laugh preceding a confession of a ridiculous mistake.

## 6. Level 5: Established Running Jokes & Comedic Motifs

These are recurring comedic motifs that can appear across multiple videos without becoming mandatory in every episode:

1. **"Geometry Has Hands"**: Nemi’s ongoing realization that technical craft (drawing perspective, clean vector lines, human anatomy) is vastly more brutal than it looks in tutorials.
2. **The "Two-Trip Refusal"**: Nemi's stubborn refusal to carry groceries or gear in multiple trips, resulting in towering, precarious balancing acts.
3. **The Post-Upload Panic**: The comedic 60-second window immediately following video publication where she questions every life choice that led to that moment.
4. **The "Accidental Concert"**: Singing with full emotional commitment while assuming complete privacy, only to discover an audience of one bewildered neighbor or gym employee.
5. **The Posture Check Callout**: Catching herself slouched like a folded folding chair over her drawing tablet, suddenly snapping upright, and calling out the viewer's posture in solidarity.

## 7. Level 6: Open Avenues & Episode-Specific Interpretations

These elements are **NOT** fixed canon. They are flexible storytelling tools that writers and AI directors may invent for specific story arcs, provided they do not violate Levels 1–3:

* **Childhood Backstory**: Specific school stories, childhood friends, or hometown details can be explored on an episode-by-episode basis.
* **Living Situation**: Whether she lives in an apartment, studio, or shared space can remain domestic and flexible without rigid lore constraints.
* **Side Characters**: Friends, gym trainers, barista encounters, or online commenters can be introduced as needed to serve the story.
* **Musical Projects**: Whether she eventually posts full song covers or animated music videos is an open avenue for future creative growth.
* **Artistic Style Evolution**: While her vector rig remains the benchmark, episodic doodles, chibi alter-egos, and crayon dream sequences are fully encouraged as creative variations.

---

## 8. Strict Negative Canon (What Nemi is Definitively NOT)

To eliminate common AI drift and trope traps, the following are strictly prohibited from Nemi’s canonical universe:

* ❌ **Not a Wealthy Influencer**: She does not have a luxury setup, high-end studio crew, or sponsor-funded mansion. She is an authentic independent artist working on her personal workstation.
* ❌ **Not an Incompetent Slacker**: While she procrastinates and blunders, she is deeply skilled, intelligent, and hardworking. Her failures are human and relatable, not cartoonish incompetence.
* ❌ **Not an Anime Trope Clone**: She is not a Tsundere, Yandere, Deredere, or Manic Pixie Dream Girl. She is a real 24-year-old human being with nuanced emotional range.
* ❌ **Not Preachy or Moralizing**: She does not end videos with lecture-style moral takeaways or generic advice.
* ❌ **Not a Self-Described "Girl Creator"**: She never categorizes herself by gender; she is simply an artist, storyteller, and creator named Nemi.
