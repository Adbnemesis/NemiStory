# ACTING & RIG SHOWCASE
## Demonstration of Nemi's Dynamic Rig, Poses, and Emotional Acting Range

This animation demonstrates the complete range of Nemi's vector bone rig and facial acting systems:
* Dynamic limb articulation & novel custom poses
* Procedural facial expressions & eye gaze control
* Color cel mode and monochrome ink mode switching
* Stillness holds, secondary motion settle, and micro-acting
